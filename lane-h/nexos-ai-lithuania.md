## One-liner

Nexos.ai, a Vilnius-based enterprise AI orchestration platform founded in 2024 by Nord Security veterans Tomas Okmanas and Eimantas Sabaliauskas, closed a €30M Series A in October 2025 (co-led by Evantic Capital and Index Ventures) to help enterprises tackle "Shadow AI" — unauthorized, ungoverned AI tool usage within organizations.

## What happened

Nexos.ai built an all-in-one platform for secure, organization-wide AI adoption, combining a web-based "AI Workspace" for employees with an "AI Gateway" for developers, giving companies a unified interface to over 200 AI models from providers including OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, and Meta — rather than employees independently adopting scattered, unsanctioned AI tools (the "Shadow AI" problem the company explicitly targets). The founders, Tomas Okmanas and Eimantas Sabaliauskas, previously built Nord Security (the company behind NordVPN), bringing established enterprise cybersecurity and privacy-product experience directly into the AI governance space.

The company progressed rapidly through two funding rounds within roughly nine months: an initial $8M round led by Index Ventures with Creandum and Dig Ventures in early 2025, followed by the €30M ($32M+) Series A in October 2025, co-led by new investor Evantic Capital and existing backer Index Ventures. The Series A capital is earmarked for enhancing the AI platform's capabilities — including advanced routing algorithms and private model deployment — and expanding across Europe and North America.

## Key decisions / turning points

- Founded by operators (Tomas Okmanas, Eimantas Sabaliauskas) with direct prior experience building and scaling a major cybersecurity/privacy product (Nord Security/NordVPN), bringing credible enterprise-trust-building expertise into a new but adjacent category (AI governance).
- Targeted "Shadow AI" specifically — unauthorized, ungoverned employee AI tool usage — as the core enterprise pain point, rather than building a generic AI aggregation or chatbot product, giving the platform a clear governance and compliance value proposition for enterprise buyers.
- Raised two funding rounds within roughly nine months (an $8M round in early 2025, then €30M in October 2025), using rapid follow-on fundraising to capitalize on accelerating enterprise AI adoption trends rather than waiting the more typical 18-24 months between rounds.
- Aggregated access to 200+ AI models from multiple leading providers into a single governed interface, positioning the platform as provider-agnostic infrastructure rather than tying enterprise customers to any single AI vendor.

## Why it matters (pattern / lesson)

Nexos.ai demonstrates that founders who've already built and scaled one major enterprise security/privacy product can move quickly into an adjacent, newly emerging governance category (AI oversight) and secure rapid, escalating institutional investment by directly addressing a fast-growing enterprise pain point (Shadow AI) rather than building generic infrastructure. Raising sequential rounds within months, rather than the traditional multi-year gap between Series A stages, also reflects how quickly capital is willing to move for enterprise AI infrastructure when a company demonstrates accelerating traction in a category investors view as urgently needed.
