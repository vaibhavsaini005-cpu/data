## One-liner

E387, founded in Sarajevo in January 2023, became Bosnia and Herzegovina's first domestic manufacturer of electric vehicle chargers, building EV charging infrastructure aimed at accelerating electric-vehicle adoption in a market with minimal existing charging network coverage.

## What happened

E387 set out to design and manufacture EV chargers domestically in Bosnia and Herzegovina, positioning itself as the country's pioneer in a category with essentially no local precedent — the country's EV charging infrastructure was, at the company's founding, extremely limited, making both the technical build and the market-education challenge significant for a first mover. The company's initial development phase was self-funded with an approximately €30,000 investment covering research and development, prototype construction, engineering services, and building out the company's public-facing presence.

By late 2023, E387 had been recognized by Bosnia and Herzegovina's Foreign Investment Promotion Agency (FIPA) as a notable domestic manufacturing venture in the "production of world class electric vehicle charging solutions" category, and was featured among EU-Startups' curated list of fast-growing Bosnian startups worth watching — external validation from both a national investment-promotion body and an established European startup-media outlet within the company's first year of operation.

## Key decisions / turning points

- Chose to manufacture EV chargers domestically in Bosnia and Herzegovina rather than importing existing charging hardware from established EU or global manufacturers, building genuine local production capability in a category with essentially no domestic precedent.
- Self-funded the initial research, prototyping, and engineering phase rather than seeking external investment from day one, retaining full ownership and control during the highest-risk early product-development stage.
- Positioned the company explicitly as a "world class" charging solutions producer per FIPA's characterization, signaling ambition to meet international quality standards rather than building a domestically-scoped, lower-specification product.
- Entered a market segment (EV charging infrastructure) directly tied to the broader, still-nascent electric-vehicle adoption curve in Bosnia and Herzegovina, betting on infrastructure build-out preceding or accompanying rather than lagging behind vehicle adoption.

## Why it matters (pattern / lesson)

E387 demonstrates that genuine infrastructure-manufacturing startups can emerge even in markets with minimal existing category precedent — Bosnia and Herzegovina had essentially no domestic EV-charger manufacturing before the company's founding — when founders are willing to self-fund the highest-risk early technical development themselves rather than waiting for external capital to de-risk the opportunity first. Recognition from both a national investment-promotion agency and international startup media within the company's first year also illustrates how a genuinely novel domestic-manufacturing bet in an emerging category (clean-energy infrastructure) can attract institutional attention quickly, even in a smaller Balkan tech ecosystem that receives comparatively little international VC coverage.
