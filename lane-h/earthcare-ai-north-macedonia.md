## One-liner

Founders Gorjan Jovanovski and Dragan pivoted their Skopje-based air-quality mapping app AirCare into EarthCare.ai, a climate intelligence platform for businesses, after discovering insurance companies wanted bulk climate data — securing a €350K investment from South Central Ventures and Zephyr Angel Group in early 2025.

## What happened

The founders originally built AirCare, a mobile app mapping and visualizing air pollution across the Balkans using open data from government sources and volunteer-run air quality sensors — a consumer-facing environmental awareness tool addressing North Macedonia's well-documented air pollution problem. During a Founders Retreat in North Macedonia, guided by experts from the Swiss Entrepreneurship Program, the team began working toward qualifying for Techstars Berlin, spending evenings locked in a hotel room refining their business model rather than socializing with other founders at the retreat.

Through dozens of customer conversations, the team discovered that insurance companies — not individual consumers — were the segment genuinely willing to pay for climate data at scale, specifically bulk, aggregated climate risk information rather than a consumer air-quality app. This insight drove the pivot from AirCare to EarthCare.ai, a platform that aggregates climate data and transforms it into actionable business insights. The pivot paid off: the team started 2025 by securing multiple new enterprise clients alongside a €350K investment from regional VC fund South Central Ventures and the Zephyr Angel Group.

## Key decisions / turning points

- Conducted dozens of customer conversations before committing to the pivot, rather than assuming the original consumer-facing air-quality app was the right long-term product, directly surfacing the insurance-industry demand signal that reshaped the business.
- Pivoted from a free-to-use consumer environmental app (AirCare) to a paid B2B climate intelligence platform (EarthCare.ai), moving from a model with limited monetization potential to one aligned with a customer segment (insurers) that had clear willingness to pay.
- Used a structured founder retreat and external mentorship (Swiss Entrepreneurship Program experts) as the catalyst for intensive strategic reworking, rather than treating pivot decisions as something to figure out alone in day-to-day operations.
- Targeted Techstars Berlin as a specific external validation and acceleration milestone, using that goal to focus and accelerate the pivot process during the retreat.

## Why it matters (pattern / lesson)

EarthCare.ai's origin story demonstrates a classic and effective pivot pattern: starting with a mission-driven consumer product (air-quality awareness) that surfaces real underlying data value, then discovering through direct customer conversations that a different segment (insurers needing bulk climate risk data) is where genuine willingness to pay exists, and re-architecting the business around that segment. It also illustrates how climate and environmental data — collected initially for public-awareness purposes — can become a monetizable B2B asset once matched to the right buyer, a pattern with broad applicability for environmental and civic-data startups struggling to monetize purely consumer-facing products.
