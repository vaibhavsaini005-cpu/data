## One-liner

Mobilly, Latvia's leading mobile payment platform for urban services with more than 500,000 users, secured €4 million in development financing from Signet Bank in December 2025 to expand its cashless payment offerings and grow into Lithuania.

## What happened

Mobilly built a mobile payment platform used daily by Latvians to pay for parking, public and intercity transport, museum visits, and other everyday urban services — positioning itself as core payments infrastructure for city life rather than a narrow single-use-case app. Anywhere mobile communications are available, the platform lets users make payments, giving it broad reach across urban and semi-urban areas.

The December 2025 €4 million financing from Signet Bank, a Latvian bank, was structured as development funding rather than a traditional equity venture round, reflecting Mobilly's position as an established, revenue-generating market leader (over 500,000 users) rather than an early-stage company needing venture-style risk capital. The capital supports continued development of secure, user-friendly cashless payment solutions and — per Civinity Group, which had earlier acquired a stake in Mobilly — the platform's expansion as an "urban services super-app" into Lithuania, where it will offer parking and EV-charging payments, public transport ticket purchases, and other services.

## Key decisions / turning points

- Built as multi-service urban payments infrastructure (parking, transport, museums) rather than a single-vertical app, creating daily-use stickiness across many different payment occasions for the same user base.
- Attracted a strategic stake acquisition from Civinity Group, positioning the company for coordinated regional expansion rather than remaining a Latvia-only standalone business.
- Financed its growth phase through debt-style development funding from a local bank (Signet Bank) rather than a traditional VC equity round, reflecting confidence in predictable cash flows from an already-profitable, high-usage payments business.
- Chose Lithuania as its first cross-border expansion market, extending the same "urban services super-app" model (parking, EV charging, transit ticketing) to a neighboring Baltic market rather than a more distant geography.

## Why it matters (pattern / lesson)

Mobilly demonstrates that once a mobile payments platform achieves genuine daily-use scale across multiple urban service categories (parking, transit, cultural venues), it can access growth capital through bank development financing rather than needing to rely solely on dilutive venture equity — a viable path for infrastructure-like fintechs with predictable revenue. Its regional expansion into Lithuania following a strategic stake acquisition also illustrates a common Baltic market pattern: a dominant single-country urban-payments platform extending its proven model into neighboring markets once it has established scale and a well-capitalized strategic partner at home.
