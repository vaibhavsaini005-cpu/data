## One-liner

Guideless, a Vilnius-based AI startup that turns recorded software workflows into narrated, multi-language training guides, raised €1 million in pre-seed funding in August 2026 led by Superhero Capital, with backing from FIRSTPICK and three Vinted leadership figures, already serving 3,000+ users across 15 markets.

## What happened

Guideless built software that records a user completing a task inside a business application and automatically converts that recorded workflow into an editable training guide — combining written instructions, visual annotations, transcripts, and AI-generated voice narration in more than 40 languages. This addresses a persistent enterprise pain point: keeping software training and documentation materials current as internal tools and workflows change, a task traditionally requiring manual screen-recording and technical-writing effort that quickly goes stale.

The €1 million pre-seed round, announced in August 2026, was led by Superhero Capital with participation from FIRSTPICK and three notable angel investors tied to Lithuanian unicorn Vinted: Group CEO Thomas Plantenga, Vinted Go CEO Vytautas Atkočaitis, and Vinted co-founder Mantas Mikuckas. That the round attracted senior leadership from Lithuania's most prominent tech success story as individual angel investors reflects strong local ecosystem validation. The company already counted Vinted itself, Masan Consumer Holdings, and the Nevada Hospital Association among its customers prior to the raise, with funds earmarked for product development, hiring, and expansion into the UK, wider Europe, and the US.

## Key decisions / turning points

- Focused narrowly on automating software-training-guide creation from recorded workflows, addressing a specific, recurring enterprise documentation cost rather than building a broad generic AI content tool.
- Built multi-language narration support (40+ languages) into the core product early, positioning the tool for multinational enterprise customers from the outset rather than a single-language market.
- Attracted angel investment specifically from executives at Vinted — Lithuania's best-known tech unicorn — lending credibility and signaling strong builder-to-builder confidence within the local ecosystem.
- Secured named enterprise customers (Vinted, Masan Consumer Holdings, Nevada Hospital Association) across different industries and geographies before the round closed, demonstrating cross-sector product-market fit at the pre-seed stage.

## Why it matters (pattern / lesson)

Guideless illustrates how Lithuania's startup ecosystem is increasingly self-reinforcing: a first generation of successful local companies like Vinted is now producing angel investors and validators for the next generation of Lithuanian startups, rather than relying solely on outside capital for credibility. The company's product also reflects a broader pattern in enterprise AI tooling — targeting narrow, high-frequency operational pain points (like documentation upkeep) with automation, where the value proposition is immediately measurable in hours saved, rather than pursuing a more diffuse "AI for everything" positioning.
