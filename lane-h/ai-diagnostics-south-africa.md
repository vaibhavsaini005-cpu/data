## One-liner

Cape Town-based AI Diagnostics raised $5.2M (R85M) in a pre-Series A round led by The Steele Foundation for Hope in April 2026, to scale deployment of its Ostium AI-powered digital stethoscope that screens for tuberculosis in real time without lab infrastructure.

## What happened

AI Diagnostics built the Ostium digital stethoscope paired with AI.TB software, an acoustic AI diagnostic tool that lets frontline healthcare workers screen for tuberculosis in real time, without requiring traditional laboratory infrastructure or specialist radiology equipment. This directly targets a core barrier to TB control in resource-constrained settings: conventional TB diagnosis typically requires lab-based testing or imaging that isn't readily accessible in many frontline clinics, especially in rural or under-resourced areas across Africa where TB burden remains high.

The $5.2M pre-Series A round, announced in mid-April 2026, was led by The Steele Foundation for Hope, with participation from the iFSP Group, Global Innovation Fund, and follow-on investment from early angel backers. The funding is earmarked to accelerate deployment of the Ostium device and its underlying AI software to more frontline health workers, scaling access to earlier, more accessible TB detection.

## Key decisions / turning points

- Built the diagnostic tool around a familiar, low-cost form factor (a stethoscope) rather than requiring new specialized hardware, lowering the adoption barrier for frontline health workers already trained on conventional medical instruments.
- Focused specifically on tuberculosis as the initial disease target rather than a broader diagnostic platform, addressing a disease with both high public-health burden and a clear unmet need for accessible, infrastructure-light screening tools.
- Attracted funding specifically from an impact-oriented foundation (The Steele Foundation for Hope) and a global innovation fund rather than purely commercial venture capital, aligning its investor base with the public-health mission of the product.
- Designed the AI software to operate without dependency on laboratory infrastructure, directly targeting deployment in the resource-constrained frontline settings where TB screening gaps are most acute.

## Why it matters (pattern / lesson)

AI Diagnostics demonstrates how pairing AI software with a familiar, low-cost medical device form factor (a stethoscope) can dramatically lower the adoption barrier for diagnostic innovation in resource-constrained healthcare settings — frontline workers don't need new equipment training, just an AI layer on top of tools they already use. Attracting impact-focused foundation and innovation-fund capital, rather than purely commercial VC, is also a viable and often more patient funding path for medtech ventures targeting public-health problems in underserved markets where near-term commercial returns may be less certain than the underlying social impact.
