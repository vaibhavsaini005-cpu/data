## One-liner

Co-founder and CEO Stefan Batory launched Booksy Pay in Poland in October 2025 to make payments a second major revenue engine; in testing, 87% of eligible customers prepaid and one in three transactions included a tip.

## What happened

Booksy had grown beyond appointment scheduling into a platform for beauty and wellness businesses. Batory decided to unify its existing payment tools into Booksy Pay, letting customers pay before, during or after an appointment inside the app.

The company launched first in Poland, planned a phased rollout across its markets and offered providers the service free for the first year. Batory said payments then generated only 5% of Booksy's revenue, but his long-term target was for payments and related products to match subscription revenue.

## Key decisions / turning points

- In October 2025, Batory chose Poland as the first market for Booksy Pay before a broader rollout, including the United States in 2026.
- He used a free first year to reduce adoption friction for service providers.
- Tests showed 87% of customers with the option chose prepayment, while customers left a tip in one-third of Booksy Pay transactions.
- The launch built on a platform that processed nearly 265 million appointments worldwide in 2024; Booksy had also reported 40% revenue growth and profitability for 2023.

## Why it matters (pattern / lesson)

A successful vertical marketplace can add a payments layer where it already controls the booking moment. The test data gave Booksy evidence that the new product solved cancellation risk for providers and payment friction for customers before it committed to a global rollout.
