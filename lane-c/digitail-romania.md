## One-liner

Digitail, a Romanian-founded AI-native operating system for veterinary clinics, raised a $23 million (€19.9 million) Series B in November 2025, led by Five Elms Capital with continued backing from Atomico and Partech, to accelerate AI capabilities and scale globally.

## What happened

Digitail built practice management software specifically for veterinary clinics, applying AI to a healthcare vertical (animal care) that has historically lagged behind human healthcare in software sophistication. Rather than adapting generic practice-management tools, the company built its platform as an "AI-native operating system," positioning AI as core infrastructure rather than a bolt-on feature to an existing workflow tool.

The November 2025 Series B, led by Five Elms Capital with participation from existing investors Atomico, Partech, Byfounders, and Gradient, followed an earlier $11 million Series A led by Atomico — demonstrating sustained investor confidence across multiple funding rounds. The continuity of investor participation across rounds (Atomico backing both the Series A and Series B) reflects strong ongoing conviction in the company's execution and market opportunity. The new capital is earmarked for accelerating product development, expanding AI capabilities, and scaling global operations beyond Digitail's initial markets.

## Key decisions / turning points

- Built specifically for veterinary practices rather than adapting general medical practice-management software, addressing workflow and clinical documentation needs specific to animal healthcare that generic tools don't capture well.
- Positioned AI as native, foundational infrastructure within the operating system rather than an add-on feature, differentiating from legacy veterinary software providers retrofitting AI onto older platforms.
- Retained the same lead investor relationship (Atomico) across both Series A and Series B rounds, reflecting sustained confidence that likely eased the fundraising process and signaled strong execution to new investors like Five Elms Capital.
- Originated from Romania while building a globally-scalable product from inception, rather than remaining focused solely on the domestic or regional veterinary market, positioning for international expansion from an early stage.

## Why it matters (pattern / lesson)

Digitail demonstrates that underserved healthcare-adjacent verticals — in this case, veterinary practice management, which has historically received less software investment than human healthcare — can support venture-scale businesses when a company builds AI-native infrastructure specifically tailored to that vertical's workflows rather than retrofitting generic tools. The company's Romanian origin combined with global-from-inception ambition also reflects a broader pattern in Central and Eastern European tech: founders building category-specific vertical software with international market ambitions from day one, rather than treating the home market as the primary long-term focus.
