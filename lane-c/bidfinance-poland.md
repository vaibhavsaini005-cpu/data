## One-liner

BidFinance, a Warsaw-based fintech founded in 2022 by Zhenia Karastyanov that digitizes the trading of debt portfolios, secured €1.6 million in seed funding in April 2025 from 4growth VC, FundingBox, Digital Ocean Ventures, and business angels to fund international expansion into the Nordic countries.

## What happened

BidFinance built an auction platform that digitizes and standardizes the process of trading debt portfolios, connecting sellers — banks, loan companies, telecoms, leasing firms, and energy companies — with buyers such as collection agencies, investment funds, and law firms. This addressed a structural inefficiency in a market where debt portfolio trading had traditionally relied on fragmented, manual, and often opaque negotiation processes between sellers and specialized buyers, rather than a standardized, transparent digital marketplace.

Since its 2022 founding, BidFinance had completed over 70 transactions with listed debt portfolios totaling more than €280 million (PLN 1.2 billion), demonstrating meaningful commercial traction in the Polish market before this April 2025 seed round. The funding, backed by 4growth VC (a Warsaw-based fintech-focused VC), FundingBox, Digital Ocean Ventures, and business angels, was explicitly earmarked to fuel international expansion — with the Nordic countries identified as the first target market, where annual unsecured debt sales are estimated at €8-10 billion, representing a substantially larger addressable market than BidFinance's home Polish market.

## Key decisions / turning points

- Built a specialized, standardized digital marketplace specifically for debt portfolio trading rather than a broader fintech lending or credit product, addressing a narrow but genuine structural inefficiency in an underserved B2B financial niche.
- Achieved substantial transaction volume (over 70 transactions, €280 million in listed portfolios) in the domestic Polish market before raising this seed round, building commercial proof points ahead of the fundraise.
- Explicitly targeted the Nordic countries as its first international expansion market based on the region's significantly larger unsecured debt sales volume (€8-10 billion annually), rather than expanding into markets with less quantified opportunity.
- Attracted a syndicate of fintech-specialist investors (4growth VC) alongside more generalist and strategic backers (FundingBox, Digital Ocean Ventures), combining sector expertise with broader venture support.

## Why it matters (pattern / lesson)

BidFinance demonstrates how narrow, B2B-focused fintech infrastructure businesses addressing genuine market inefficiencies — in this case, the historically manual and fragmented process of trading debt portfolios — can build meaningful transaction volume in a home market before using that traction to justify international expansion into markets with quantifiably larger addressable opportunity. The company's explicit, data-driven selection of the Nordic countries as its expansion target (based on debt-sales volume estimates rather than geographic proximity or convenience) also illustrates a disciplined, market-size-first approach to international expansion that smaller European fintech companies can use to prioritize where to deploy limited expansion capital.
