## One-liner

FRVR, a Lisbon-headquartered gaming platform founded by industry veterans Chris Benjaminsen and Brian Meidell, raised $12.7M (bringing total funding past $100M) in August 2024 to advance its AI game-creation engine, Forge, which lets both developers and non-developers build and distribute games across mobile, PC, and web.

## What happened

FRVR, founded in 2017, built a gaming distribution platform combined with Forge, an AI-powered game development tool designed to let developers build faster and non-developers create and distribute games without traditional coding expertise, democratizing game creation across mobile, PC, and web channels simultaneously. This dual focus — both distribution infrastructure and AI-accelerated creation tools — positioned FRVR as infrastructure spanning the full game-development-to-distribution pipeline rather than specializing in just one stage.

The August 2024 $12.7M round, led by Iberis Capital with participation from Indico Capital Partners, Lince Capital, and returning investor Hiro Capital, brought FRVR's cumulative funding past $100M — a substantial capital base for a gaming infrastructure company reflecting sustained investor confidence across multiple funding cycles since the company's 2017 founding. The capital is earmarked for advancing the Forge AI engine toward more personalized gaming experiences and scaling a creator economy empowering game creators globally.

## Key decisions / turning points

- Built both distribution infrastructure and an AI-powered creation tool (Forge) as a combined offering, rather than specializing in only one part of the game development pipeline, capturing value across the full creator journey from building to publishing.
- Explicitly targeted non-developers as creators, not just professional game studios, using AI to lower the technical barrier to game creation and expanding the addressable creator base beyond traditional development teams.
- Sustained multiple funding rounds over roughly seven years (2017-2024) to reach over $100M in cumulative funding, reflecting a long-term capital-intensive infrastructure build rather than a quick-growth, quick-exit trajectory.
- Attracted a syndicate blending Portuguese/regional funds (Iberis Capital, Indico Capital Partners, Lince Capital) with an international gaming-focused investor (Hiro Capital), combining local market knowledge with sector-specific expertise.

## Why it matters (pattern / lesson)

FRVR demonstrates that combining AI-accelerated content creation tools with existing distribution infrastructure can create a defensible, full-pipeline platform in the gaming industry — rather than competing purely on either creation tools or distribution reach alone, the combination lets the company capture value at multiple points in a creator's journey. Democratizing game creation specifically for non-developers via AI also reflects a broader pattern across creative-tool categories: AI lowers the technical skill barrier to content creation, expanding a platform's addressable creator base well beyond those with traditional technical training.
