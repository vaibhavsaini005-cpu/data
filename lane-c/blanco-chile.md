## One-liner

Blanco, a Chilean fintech founded in 2024 that runs an AI-native invoice-factoring platform generating $8.4M in annualized revenue and moving over $33B in invoice stock, raised a $5.2M seed round led by Krealo (Credicorp's venture arm) in July 2026.

## What happened

Blanco, founded by Cristóbal Contreras, Diego Contreras, and Andrés Hortal, built a factoring platform for Chilean SMEs that connects directly to Chile's tax authority to automatically detect invoice emissions, then runs the full enrollment, risk assessment, pricing, and collections flow through AI agents — sending financing simulations to clients via WhatsApp without human intervention. This automated approach directly targets the labor-intensive, manual underwriting process that typically constrains how quickly and cheaply traditional factoring providers can serve SME customers.

The July 2026 $5.2M seed round was led by Krealo, the venture arm of Credicorp — Peru's largest financial group — a strategic investor whose regional network opens a natural expansion path for Blanco into Peru and Colombia beyond its Chilean base. By the time of the raise, the company was already generating $8.4M in annualized revenue and moving over $33B in invoice stock, substantial commercial metrics for a company founded just two years earlier.

## Key decisions / turning points

- Built direct integration with Chile's tax authority to automatically detect invoice emissions, removing a manual data-entry step that typically slows down traditional factoring onboarding and reduces the speed advantage AI automation could otherwise provide.
- Automated the full risk assessment, pricing, and collections workflow through AI agents rather than human underwriters, enabling the company to serve SME customers at a cost structure traditional factoring providers cannot match.
- Delivered financing simulations via WhatsApp specifically, meeting Chilean SME customers on the messaging platform they already use rather than requiring adoption of a separate app or portal.
- Raised from Krealo, a strategic investor with an explicit regional network (Credicorp spans Peru, Colombia, and beyond), positioning the seed round as a platform for multi-country expansion rather than purely Chilean-market capital.

## Why it matters (pattern / lesson)

Blanco demonstrates that AI-native automation of traditionally labor-intensive financial services (invoice factoring underwriting) can generate substantial revenue ($8.4M ARR) and transaction volume ($33B+ invoice stock) within just two years of founding, when the automation directly removes the specific bottlenecks (manual data entry, human underwriting) that constrain traditional providers. Raising from a strategic financial-group venture arm with an explicit regional footprint (Krealo/Credicorp) is also a reusable pattern for fintechs seeking capital that comes bundled with a credible path to multi-country expansion, rather than treating geographic growth as a separate challenge to solve after fundraising.
