## One-liner

Belo, a Buenos Aires-based crypto wallet serving over 3 million users across Latin America, raised a $14 million Series A led by stablecoin issuer Tether in April 2026, to expand stablecoin-based payments into Mexico, Chile, Colombia, Peru, Bolivia, and Paraguay.

## What happened

Belo, founded in 2021, built a crypto wallet supporting both local currency and digital-dollar (stablecoin) transfers, addressing a well-established demand pattern in Argentina and across Latin America: using dollar-pegged stablecoins as a hedge against local currency volatility and inflation, particularly acute in Argentina's economic context. The wallet's growth to over 3 million users reflects genuine product-market fit for stablecoin-based financial tools in a region where currency instability makes dollar access a persistent consumer priority.

The April 2026 $14 million Series A, led by Tether — the issuer of the world's largest stablecoin, USDT — with participation from Titan Fund, The Venture City, Mindset Ventures, and G2, followed an earlier $3 million seed round in May 2022. Tether's decision to lead the round is strategically notable: as the dominant stablecoin issuer, Tether has a direct commercial interest in expanding real-world stablecoin payment rails, making Belo's wallet infrastructure a natural investment target rather than a purely financial-return-driven bet. The capital is earmarked specifically for expanding Belo's stablecoin payments infrastructure into six additional Latin American markets: Mexico, Chile, Colombia, Peru, Bolivia, and Paraguay.

## Key decisions / turning points

- Built the wallet around both local-currency and stablecoin transfers from inception, directly addressing Argentina's specific currency-instability context rather than launching as a generic crypto wallet without local-market tailoring.
- Attracted Tether as lead investor for the Series A — a strategic rather than purely financial investor with direct commercial incentive in stablecoin payment-rail expansion, potentially bringing distribution and liquidity advantages beyond capital alone.
- Sequenced funding from a modest $3M seed (2022) to a substantially larger $14M Series A (2026), reflecting multi-year platform validation (3 million users) before scaling fundraising size.
- Prioritized six new Latin American markets simultaneously for the Series A expansion, rather than a single adjacent market, betting that the currency-instability use case generalizes broadly across the region rather than being uniquely Argentina-specific.

## Why it matters (pattern / lesson)

Belo demonstrates that crypto/stablecoin wallets solving a genuine macroeconomic pain point — currency instability and dollar access — can achieve substantial organic user growth (3 million+ users) in Latin America, a pattern distinct from crypto adoption driven primarily by speculation in more currency-stable markets. Tether's strategic decision to lead the round also illustrates a broader trend of stablecoin issuers investing directly in payment infrastructure and wallet companies that expand real-world stablecoin usage, rather than remaining purely a backend issuer — a vertical-integration strategy that deepens the issuer's stake in the broader stablecoin payments ecosystem.
