## One-liner

Peruvian point-of-sale payment hardware provider VendeMás, founded in 2017, was acquired from Niubiz by Colombian fintech Bold in October 2025, marking Bold's entry into the Peruvian market through an established local player rather than a from-scratch launch.

## What happened

VendeMás built physical point-of-sale (POS) hardware and payment-acceptance technology for small and medium merchants in Peru — infrastructure enabling businesses to accept card and digital payments — operating for years without raising traditional venture funding rounds, instead growing under ownership by Niubiz, Peru's dominant payment-processing network. This ownership structure gave VendeMás distribution and market credibility that a typical unfunded startup would lack, while still leaving it exposed to strategic decisions made by its parent.

In October 2025, Colombian fintech Bold acquired VendeMás from Niubiz, using the acquisition as its entry point into the Peruvian market. Rather than building payment-acceptance infrastructure and merchant relationships from zero in a new country, Bold bought an established local operator with existing merchant relationships, hardware distribution, and market knowledge — a build-via-acquisition market-entry strategy increasingly common among Latin American fintechs expanding regionally.

## Key decisions / turning points

- Grew for years as an unfunded, corporate-backed operator (owned by Niubiz) rather than pursuing independent venture capital, trading founder/investor upside for the stability and distribution access of a large parent's balance sheet.
- Niubiz's decision to divest VendeMás to Bold reflects a strategic choice to monetize a non-core asset rather than continue operating a POS hardware subsidiary alongside its core payment-network business.
- Bold chose acquisition over organic market entry for its Peru expansion, prioritizing speed-to-market and existing merchant relationships over the lower upfront cost of building independently.

## Why it matters (pattern / lesson)

VendeMás's acquisition illustrates a market-entry pattern specific to Latin American fintech expansion: rather than each regional fintech launching independently in every new country, established local payment-infrastructure operators — even unfunded, corporate-owned ones — become acquisition targets for fintechs seeking fast, low-risk entry into new markets with pre-built merchant networks. It also shows that "unfunded" doesn't mean undervalued: an unfunded operator with strong distribution can still be a strategically valuable acquisition target for a well-capitalized regional player.
