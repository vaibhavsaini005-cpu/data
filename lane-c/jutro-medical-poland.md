## One-liner

Warsaw-based Jutro Medical, an AI-first primary care operator running 20 clinics across Poland serving roughly 120,000 patients, raised a €24M Series A extension in December 2025 (bringing total Series A funding to €36M) led by Warsaw Equity Group to scale its clinic rollup strategy and AI-supported care operations.

## What happened

Jutro Medical built an integrated primary-care operator model combining physical clinics with online care and AI-assisted clinical operations, growing from a single clinic to a network of 20 clinics across Poland. The company reported 270% year-over-year growth, with its AI systems now supporting nearly 1,500 doctor visits per month — indicating AI is embedded directly into day-to-day clinical workflow rather than functioning as a peripheral feature.

The €24M December 2025 extension, layered onto an earlier €12M tranche from March 2025 to bring total Series A funding to €36M, was led by Warsaw Equity Group with participation from Vinci, naturalX Health Ventures, Fluent Ventures, Aternus, KAYA VC, and Inovo VC, plus a debt component from mBank and Orbit Capital — combining equity and debt financing to fund both operational scaling and physical clinic acquisitions. The capital is earmarked for continuing the company's clinic rollup strategy in Poland and beyond, alongside further expansion of AI agents in daily primary-care operations.

## Key decisions / turning points

- Built a "rollup" strategy — acquiring and integrating individual clinics into one unified operator — rather than opening greenfield locations one at a time, allowing faster network scale-up (reaching 20 clinics) than organic buildout typically permits.
- Embedded AI directly into clinical operations (supporting nearly 1,500 doctor visits monthly) rather than positioning it as a consumer-facing chatbot or administrative tool alone, integrating the technology into the core care-delivery workflow.
- Split its Series A into two tranches (€12M in March, €24M in December of the same year) rather than raising the full amount upfront, allowing the company to demonstrate additional traction (270% YoY growth) between rounds to support a larger follow-on raise at improved terms.
- Combined equity financing with bank debt (mBank, Orbit Capital) specifically to fund clinic acquisitions, using debt for capital-intensive physical expansion while reserving equity capital for technology and operations.

## Why it matters (pattern / lesson)

Jutro Medical demonstrates that healthcare rollup strategies — acquiring and integrating existing clinics rather than building new locations from scratch — can be significantly accelerated by embedding AI directly into clinical workflows, turning acquired clinics into a more efficient, technology-enabled network faster than pure organic buildout would allow. Splitting a Series A into sequential tranches tied to demonstrated growth milestones, rather than raising it all at once, is also a reusable financing pattern: it lets a company prove out its model with initial capital before securing a larger follow-on round from many of the same investors at improved confidence and terms.
