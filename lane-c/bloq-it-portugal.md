## One-liner

Bloq.it co-founders Miha Jagodic and João Lopes raised €28 million in May 2025 to expand their parcel-locker infrastructure across Europe; by year-end revenue had risen 122% to more than €84 million and the network had expanded from three countries to nine.

## What happened

The Portuguese company supplies parcel-locker hardware, software, and field operations to logistics networks. On May 28, 2025, CEO Jagodic and COO/CRO Lopes announced a €28 million Series B to hire, enter additional European markets, and pursue acquisitions. At the time, the system was already processing more than 50 million shipments a year.

The expansion produced a measurable step-change during 2025. Bloq.it reported more than €84 million in annual revenue, up 122%; approximately 88.5 million transactions, up 280%; and lockers operating in nine countries, compared with three a year earlier. Manufacturing output increased 410%, and deployments peaked at 1,043 lockers in one month.

## Key decisions / turning points

- Jagodic and Lopes chose a vertically integrated combination of locker hardware, software, and field service rather than selling a standalone component.
- Raised €28 million specifically for team expansion, new-country launches, and M&A.
- Scaled manufacturing through four European hubs as the installed network more than doubled.
- Standardized the NEXT locker model across seven markets and four enterprise customers during its first year.

## Why it matters (pattern / lesson)

Bloq.it demonstrates that hardware-enabled infrastructure can scale like software when its design is standardized and paired with an operating platform. The 2025 financing decision was followed by simultaneous growth in revenue, countries, transactions, production, and deployments, showing that the capital expanded a functioning system rather than subsidizing an unproven rollout.
