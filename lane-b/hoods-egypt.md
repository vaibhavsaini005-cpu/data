## One-liner

Hoods founder Moustafa Hanafy took the Egyptian live-shopping app onto Shark Tank in January 2025 seeking EGP 5 million, after the product had generated more than EGP 30 million in 2024 sales, registered 150,000 users, and completed over 20,000 orders.

## What happened

Hanafy built Hoods to combine live video, creator-led product demonstrations, checkout, payments, and delivery in one commerce application. On the January 29, 2025 Shark Tank Egypt broadcast, he presented the business and sought EGP 5 million for a 5% stake, using the appearance to fund regional expansion.

The pitch followed measurable 2024 traction: more than EGP 30 million in sales, 150,000 registered users, and over 20,000 completed orders in Egypt. Hanafy also launched a Saudi pilot during 2024; within three months it recorded more than 3,000 registrations and 200 successful orders. He reported that overall sales had grown more than 400% year over year.

## Key decisions / turning points

- Hanafy designed checkout directly into live video rather than sending viewers to separate social-media and commerce systems.
- Expanded from Egypt into a Saudi pilot in 2024, testing regional demand before a full rollout.
- Used a televised January 2025 investment pitch to seek EGP 5 million for scaling.
- Kept the Saudi test deliberately small enough to measure registrations and completed orders over its first three months.

## Why it matters (pattern / lesson)

Hoods demonstrates how a regionally adapted commerce format can be validated in layers: transaction traction at home, a time-boxed test in a second market, and then outside capital for expansion. The founder tied the fundraising decision to concrete sales, user, and order evidence rather than relying only on the popularity of live shopping elsewhere.
