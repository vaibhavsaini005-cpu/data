## One-liner

Midas, an Istanbul-based retail investment platform serving 3.5 million users, closed an $80 million Series B round in August 2025 led by QED Investors — the largest fintech investment in Turkish history — bringing its total funding to over $140 million.

## What happened

Midas built a single-platform investment app giving Turkish retail investors access to Borsa Istanbul, U.S. stock markets, mutual funds, and cryptocurrencies, addressing a gap in a market where accessing international markets and diverse asset classes had previously required navigating multiple separate brokerages and platforms. The company grew to serve 3.5 million investors, positioning it as Turkey's leading consumer investment platform ahead of this record-setting funding round.

The $80 million Series B, announced August 19, 2025, was led by QED Investors — a globally respected fintech-focused fund — with a notably diverse investor syndicate that included the International Finance Corporation (a development-finance institution), HSG (formerly Sequoia China), QuantumLight (founded by Revolut CEO Nik Storonsky), Spice Expeditions LP, and individual investor George Rzepecki. The round was explicitly framed as marking Turkey's largest-ever fintech investment, with proceeds earmarked to strengthen Midas's security infrastructure to international standards and to accelerate development of advanced trading products aimed at active traders, rather than purely for user-acquisition spending.

## Key decisions / turning points

- Built a unified platform spanning domestic (Borsa Istanbul) and international (U.S. stocks, crypto, mutual funds) asset classes rather than a single-market brokerage, broadening its addressable customer base among Turkish retail investors seeking diversification.
- Attracted a genuinely international and diverse investor syndicate — combining a specialist fintech fund (QED), a development-finance institution (IFC), a repositioned Asia-focused fund (HSG), and a prominent fintech-founder-led fund (QuantumLight) — rather than relying on a narrower set of regional investors.
- Explicitly prioritized security-infrastructure investment alongside product development as a stated use of proceeds, reflecting the trust requirements of a platform handling retail investors' capital across multiple asset classes and jurisdictions.
- Achieved scale (3.5 million users) before raising this round, giving the company leverage to secure record-setting Series B terms rather than needing to raise growth capital speculatively.

## Why it matters (pattern / lesson)

Midas's record-setting round demonstrates that Turkish fintech companies can attract top-tier international venture capital — including specialist funds, development-finance institutions, and funds backed by other fintech founders — once they've achieved genuine user scale and built a differentiated, multi-asset-class product rather than a narrow single-market offering. The round's framing as Turkey's largest-ever fintech deal also signals growing international investor confidence in the broader Turkish fintech sector's maturity, following years in which large late-stage fintech rounds in the country had been comparatively rare relative to funding activity in Gulf and broader MENA markets.
