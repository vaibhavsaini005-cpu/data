## One-liner

Enakl, a Casablanca-based shared mobility startup founded in 2022 by Samir Bennani and Charles Pommarède, closed a $2.3 million seed round in December 2025 to scale its pre-planned mini-bus commuting service and launch a new SaaS offering, after securing its first public-sector pilot contract with the Casablanca–Settat Region earlier that year.

## What happened

Enakl built a shared urban transport platform offering pre-planned commuting via mini-buses that follow fixed routes, addressing Casablanca's urban mobility gap between costly private car ownership and less predictable informal or ad hoc shared transport options. By combining route-planning intelligence with a booking system for shared mini-bus seats, the company positioned itself between traditional mass transit and individual ride-hailing, targeting commuters and enterprises seeking predictable, cost-effective transport at scale.

The $2.3 million seed round, finalized in December 2025, brought together three new Moroccan investors — Azur Innovation Fund, Witamax, and MFounders — alongside returning backers Catalyst Fund and Digital Africa, reflecting continued confidence from earlier investors alongside fresh domestic capital. Notably, the round followed Enakl's 2025 win of its first public-sector pilot contract with the Casablanca–Settat Region, a milestone providing government-backed validation ahead of the private funding round and demonstrating a path to revenue beyond purely private commercial contracts.

## Key decisions / turning points

- Focused specifically on pre-planned, fixed-route shared mini-bus commuting rather than on-demand ride-hailing, targeting a distinct mobility niche between mass transit and individual ride-hailing services.
- Secured a public-sector pilot contract with the Casablanca–Settat regional government in 2025 before closing its seed round, using government validation as a credibility signal ahead of private fundraising.
- Retained existing investors (Catalyst Fund, Digital Africa) as returning backers in this round while also bringing in three new Moroccan investors, blending continuity of investor relationships with expanded domestic capital participation.
- Earmarked funding specifically for commercial team growth, testing new fleet development models, and launching a SaaS product — diversifying its business model beyond direct ridepooling operations toward software licensing.

## Why it matters (pattern / lesson)

Enakl demonstrates how urban mobility startups in emerging markets can build credibility and secure private investment by first winning public-sector pilot contracts, using government validation of a novel transport model as a signal to private investors before or alongside a funding round. The company's move to add a SaaS offering alongside its core ridepooling operations also reflects a broader pattern among mobility-infrastructure startups: diversifying revenue models to include software licensing for the underlying route-planning and fleet-management technology, rather than depending solely on direct transport-operations revenue in a single home market.
