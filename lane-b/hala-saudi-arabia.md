## One-liner

Saudi fintech HALA Payments, founded in 2018 by Esam Alnahdi and Maher Loubieh to help SMEs manage inbound and outbound payments, was selected in October 2024 to join the government-backed Saudi Unicorns Programme, positioning it for accelerated growth in the Kingdom's fintech sector.

## What happened

HALA Payments built payment-management infrastructure for small and medium enterprises, allowing SME merchants to manage both incoming and outgoing payments through a unified platform — a category central to Saudi Arabia's broader push toward reducing cash dependency and digitizing SME financial operations as part of its Vision 2030 economic diversification program. By the time of its selection to the Saudi Unicorns Programme in October 2024, the company had scaled to serve over 142,000 businesses processing more than $8 billion in annual transactions.

The Saudi Unicorns Programme itself is a government initiative specifically designed to identify and support high-growth Saudi companies with credible potential to reach unicorn valuation status, providing selected companies with government-backed resources, visibility, and support structures beyond what private capital alone typically provides. HALA's selection reflects both its transaction-volume scale and the government's confidence in fintech as a strategic growth sector within the Kingdom's economic diversification strategy.

## Key decisions / turning points

- Built infrastructure for SME payment management specifically, rather than consumer payments — targeting a segment (small business financial operations) central to Saudi Arabia's broader digitization and formalization push.
- Achieved substantial transaction-volume scale (142,000+ businesses, $8B+ annual transactions) before pursuing the highest-profile growth-support program available, using demonstrated scale as the primary qualification criterion.
- Aligned closely with Saudi Arabia's Vision 2030 economic diversification priorities (reducing cash dependency, growing the fintech sector), positioning the company favorably for government-backed growth programs beyond pure private-market fundraising.

## Why it matters (pattern / lesson)

HALA's selection into the Saudi Unicorns Programme shows how Gulf fintech companies serving strategically prioritized economic-diversification goals (SME digitization, reduced cash dependency) can access government-backed growth support as a complement to, or accelerant for, private venture funding — a pattern less available to founders in markets without equivalent national economic-transformation programs. Demonstrated transaction volume at scale, not just funding raised, was the qualifying signal here, a useful benchmark for other B2B payments infrastructure companies targeting similar government-backed growth initiatives in the Gulf region.
