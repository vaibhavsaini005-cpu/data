## One-liner

Bankly co-founder and CEO Tomilola Majekodunmi agreed to a full acquisition by C-One Ventures on April 29, 2025 after building a US$5 million-revenue fintech that processed more than US$1.2 billion annually; its licences, platform, and 60-person team transferred, and the bank remains live under the Bankly brand.

## What happened

Majekodunmi and Fredrick Adams founded Bankly to digitize informal savings and last-mile financial services in Nigeria. The business grew from a voucher-funded “recharge to save” product into a licensed payment company and microfinance bank with savings, credit, payments, agency banking, and banking-as-a-service products.

Majekodunmi chose C-One Ventures as the next owner after six years of building. C-One completed a full acquisition on April 29, 2025, covering Bankly's licences, platform, and team. Majekodunmi moved into an advisory transition role and said the deal would keep the financial-inclusion mission alive despite the challenges the independent company had faced.

Her closing figures make the outcome concrete: US$1.7 million of equity had produced about US$5 million in revenue, more than 50,000 agents, 250,000 unique banked users, over five million people served annually through the channels, and more than US$1.2 billion in annual processing volume. Bankly's current site still markets regulated personal, business, and API banking and identifies the company as a Central Bank of Nigeria-licensed microfinance bank serving more than two million individuals and enterprises.

## Key decisions / turning points

- Majekodunmi expanded Bankly from a digitized savings model into two regulated businesses: payments and microfinance banking.
- On April 29, 2025, she accepted a full-company acquisition and stayed as an adviser while C-One integrated the licences, platform, and 60-person team.
- The transaction preserved the Bankly brand and regulated operating platform rather than winding them down.
- The resolved outcome combined a closed transfer with founder-reported scale: US$5 million in revenue, more than US$1.2 billion processed annually, and over 50,000 agents.

## Why it matters (pattern / lesson)

Bankly shows that regulatory infrastructure can be as important an acquisition asset as software. Majekodunmi converted relatively little equity into licences, distribution, transaction volume, and a working team; the buyer acquired the whole system and kept it operating, making this a strategic continuation rather than a rescue announcement with no endpoint.
