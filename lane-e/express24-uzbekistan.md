## One-liner

Express24, an Uzbek on-demand delivery startup founded in 2017 delivering ready-to-eat meals, groceries, and market products, had its assets acquired by Yandex Uzbekistan in October 2024, as Yandex moved to merge the business into a unified food-delivery platform.

## What happened

Express24 built an on-demand delivery service in Uzbekistan spanning multiple product categories — ready-to-eat meals, groceries, and general market products — rather than specializing narrowly in restaurant delivery alone, positioning itself as a broader last-mile logistics platform for everyday consumer needs since its 2017 founding.

By October 2024, Yandex Uzbekistan — the local arm of Russian technology giant Yandex, already active in ride-hailing and other on-demand services in the market — acquired part of Express24's assets, with the explicit stated goal of merging the business into a unified food-delivery platform under Yandex's umbrella. Rather than a distressed shutdown, the deal represented a consolidation move in Uzbekistan's on-demand delivery market, folding an established independent local player's operations, infrastructure, and market presence into a larger regional platform's broader ecosystem.

## Key decisions / turning points

- Built a multi-category delivery platform (meals, groceries, market products) rather than a single-vertical service, which likely made Express24's infrastructure and merchant relationships more attractive as an asset for a larger platform seeking to expand across multiple delivery categories at once.
- Yandex Uzbekistan pursued an asset acquisition and merger into a unified platform rather than simply competing head-to-head with Express24 as a separate service, consolidating market share and operational infrastructure rather than duplicating logistics networks.
- The deal was structured as an asset acquisition rather than a distressed fire-sale, suggesting Express24 retained enough independent value (established merchant network, delivery infrastructure, brand recognition) to be a strategic acquisition target rather than a failure needing rescue.
- Occurred within a broader pattern of regional consolidation in Central Asian on-demand delivery markets, where larger, better-capitalized regional platforms (Yandex) absorb established local independent operators rather than each maintaining separate parallel infrastructure.

## Why it matters (pattern / lesson)

Express24's acquisition by Yandex Uzbekistan illustrates a common late-stage path for independent on-demand delivery startups in markets increasingly dominated by a small number of well-capitalized regional "super-app" platforms: rather than continuing to compete independently against a much larger rival with broader infrastructure (ride-hailing, payments, multiple delivery verticals), a locally-established player can extract value by having its assets absorbed into that larger platform's unified offering. This consolidation pattern — a regional tech giant folding a category-specific local pioneer into a broader multi-service platform — is a recurring dynamic in emerging on-demand-delivery markets where scale and cross-service bundling increasingly determine competitive outcomes.
