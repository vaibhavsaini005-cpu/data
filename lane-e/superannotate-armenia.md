## One-liner

SuperAnnotate, an AI training-data platform with core engineering roots in Yerevan, closed a $36M Series B in 2024 led by Socium Ventures with participation from NVIDIA and Databricks Ventures, capitalizing on surging enterprise demand for high-quality labeled data to train AI models.

## What happened

SuperAnnotate built tooling for data annotation and training-data management — the labeling and quality-control pipeline that enterprise machine learning teams need to prepare data before training models — positioning itself in a category that grew sharply in importance as generative AI adoption accelerated demand for well-curated training datasets. The company maintains substantial engineering operations in Yerevan, part of a broader pattern of Armenian tech companies retaining deep local R&D roots while raising capital and selling internationally.

Its 2024 Series B, backed by strategic investors NVIDIA and Databricks Ventures alongside Socium Ventures and other participants, reflected specific confidence from infrastructure-adjacent players in the AI stack — both NVIDIA (chips/compute) and Databricks (data platforms) have direct commercial interest in the data-annotation layer functioning well, since better labeled data improves the training workloads run on their own infrastructure.

## Key decisions / turning points

- Built specifically for the training-data preparation and quality-control layer of the AI pipeline rather than for model training or inference itself, positioning the company adjacent to — rather than in direct competition with — the large foundation-model labs.
- Retained substantial engineering operations in Armenia while raising from and serving an international, US-centric enterprise AI customer base, rather than relocating engineering to be closer to customers.
- Attracted strategic (not just financial) investors — NVIDIA and Databricks Ventures — whose core businesses benefit directly from better-annotated training data, aligning investor incentives with product adoption across the AI infrastructure stack.

## Why it matters (pattern / lesson)

SuperAnnotate's raise demonstrates that Armenia's tech sector can produce infrastructure-layer AI companies that compete globally by keeping engineering talent local while building customer relationships and capital relationships in international markets — a template increasingly common among Armenian tech firms, per the country's tech sector reaching $2.3B in turnover and 7% of GDP by 2024. The strategic-investor mix also shows how the AI training-data category specifically attracts backing from infrastructure incumbents (chipmakers, data platforms) whose own growth depends on the broader AI pipeline functioning well, not just from traditional venture capital.
