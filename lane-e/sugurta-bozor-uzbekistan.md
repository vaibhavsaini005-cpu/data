## One-liner

Sug'urta Bozor, Uzbekistan's first online insurance marketplace founded by three WIUT graduates, raised $220,000 from international investors and the local UzVC National Venture Capital Fund, scaling to partnerships with the country's top 10 insurance companies and roughly 200,000 users after starting with just $10,000 of the founders' own money.

## What happened

Sug'urta Bozor built a B2C and B2B2C marketplace connecting Uzbek consumers directly with insurance companies, letting users compare and purchase auto and other insurance policies online rather than navigating each insurer's offline sales process individually — while giving participating insurers an additional digital sales channel the founders say increased per-policy profit by roughly 30%. Founders Azizbek Kurbonov, Tigran Bekmullin, and Abdurauf Sherkulov bootstrapped the venture with around $10,000 of their own capital before raising outside funding, and the company earns revenue through a roughly 20% commission on each policy sold plus a per-inspection fee for vehicle checks.

Nine months after starting, the team raised its first $100,000 round from local venture fund UzVC, and went on to secure a total of $220,000 from international investors while working through nearly every accelerator program available in the region, including Plug and Play Tech Center, Aloha Ventures, Innotech, the Startup Growth Program, and IT Park Uzbekistan's own programs. By the time of these later profiles, the platform had partnered with the top 10 insurance companies operating in Uzbekistan and reached roughly 200,000 users nationwide.

## Key decisions / turning points

- Bootstrapped the first nine months on founder capital alone (~$10,000) to build a working product before approaching outside investors, giving the team leverage and validation for the subsequent UzVC round.
- Positioned the company as infrastructure serving both consumers and insurers (a two-sided marketplace) rather than a single-sided comparison site, letting it capture commission revenue from the insurer side while giving consumers free comparison tools.
- Systematically worked through nearly every accelerator program available in Uzbekistan and the broader region (Plug and Play, Aloha Ventures, Innotech, IT Park programs) rather than relying on a single investor relationship, diversifying both capital sources and mentorship.
- Focused first on securing partnerships with Uzbekistan's leading insurance companies before pursuing aggressive user-acquisition spending, building supply-side trust as a foundation for scaling demand.

## Why it matters (pattern / lesson)

Sug'urta Bozor demonstrates a capital-efficient path for insurtech marketplaces in underpenetrated Central Asian markets: bootstrap a working two-sided product first, then use that traction to unlock a sequence of small but compounding investments from local venture funds and regional accelerator programs rather than requiring a single large round upfront. The company's growth to partnerships with all of Uzbekistan's top 10 insurers and roughly 200,000 users on cumulative funding of just $220,000 illustrates how thin the capital requirements can be for a well-targeted marketplace business addressing a genuine structural gap — in this case, the lack of an accessible online channel connecting Uzbek consumers to insurance products.
