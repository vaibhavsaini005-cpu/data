## One-liner

Call2action.ai, a Kazakh generative-AI hiring platform founded by Yersultan Jussakinov that has conducted over 5,000 AI-driven interviews, closed a $500,000 pre-seed round at a $6M valuation in late October 2025 to expand its automated driver-hiring platform across the US trucking industry.

## What happened

Call2action.ai built a generative-AI hiring platform specifically targeting the US logistics and trucking industry's persistent driver, mechanic, and dispatcher hiring bottleneck — using AI to conduct interviews, assess candidate skills, and generate shortlists of qualified candidates within hours, a process the company claims is five times faster and three times more cost-effective than conventional hiring methods. Despite building the product from a Kazakhstan base, the company targeted the US market directly as its primary customer base from the outset, reflecting a "born global" approach rather than establishing domestic traction first.

The late-October 2025 $500,000 pre-seed round, raised at a $6M valuation, came predominantly from US venture funds and angel investors, including Y Combinator graduates, MIT alumni, and MOST VC — an investor base geographically aligned with the company's target market rather than its home base. By the time of the raise, the platform had already onboarded more than 30 carriers within three months of its US launch and conducted over 5,000 AI-driven interviews, with the company targeting more than 50 paying clients as it continues scaling toward a leading position in US logistics-industry AI hiring.

## Key decisions / turning points

- Targeted the US trucking and logistics industry as the primary customer market from inception, despite building and operating the core team from Kazakhstan, rather than first establishing a domestic Kazakhstani customer base.
- Raised primarily from US-based investors (Y Combinator alumni, MIT alumni, MOST VC) rather than regional Central Asian capital, aligning its investor base geographically with its target market rather than its operational base.
- Focused specifically on driver, mechanic, and dispatcher hiring within trucking and logistics, rather than building a horizontal AI hiring tool, addressing a well-documented, persistent labor shortage in US trucking with a narrowly-scoped solution.
- Achieved rapid customer onboarding (30+ carriers in three months) before and around the pre-seed raise, using early US market traction as validation for the "Kazakhstan-built, US-targeted" go-to-market approach.

## Why it matters (pattern / lesson)

Call2action.ai demonstrates a "born global" pattern increasingly viable for AI-native startups: building the product and engineering team in a lower-cost base (Kazakhstan) while targeting and raising capital directly from the highest-value customer and investor market (the US) from day one, rather than sequencing domestic traction before international expansion. Focusing narrowly on one industry's specific hiring pain point (trucking driver recruitment) within the broader AI-hiring category also illustrates how vertical specialization can help an early-stage company demonstrate concrete customer traction (30+ carriers, 5,000+ interviews) quickly enough to support institutional fundraising within months of launch.
