## One-liner

Jalat Logistics, a Cambodian last-mile delivery platform founded by three Forbes 30 Under 30 Asia entrepreneurs and serving over 6,500 merchants with 1M+ completed deliveries, became the debut investment of Canadia Group's newly launched Canadia Impact Fund in November 2025.

## What happened

Jalat Logistics built a last-mile delivery and logistics platform serving thousands of merchants across Cambodia, growing to more than 6,500 merchant partners and completing over one million deliveries nationwide — becoming one of the country's leading local delivery platforms in a market where e-commerce and delivery infrastructure has been rapidly expanding, partly spurred by Grab's 2024 acquisition of Cambodian super-app Nham24 and new infrastructure like OCIC's Techo International Airport.

In November 2025, Jalat became the first investment of the Canadia Impact Fund Co (CIFC), a newly launched corporate venture capital vehicle from Canadia Group — one of Cambodia's oldest and most diversified conglomerates. CIFC is led by CEO Thierry Tea, a former head of Airbus Helicopters Philippines with a background investing in mobility, enterprise software, and fintech, and targets early-stage deals from pre-seed to Series A across education, healthcare, logistics, digital infrastructure, and renewable energy — positioning Jalat's investment as a flagship deal establishing the fund's logistics-sector thesis.

## Key decisions / turning points

- Built merchant density (6,500+ partners) and delivery volume (1M+ completed deliveries) as the core proof points demonstrating market traction, giving a first-time corporate VC fund clear, quantifiable evidence to back its debut investment decision.
- Attracted investment specifically from a large domestic conglomerate's new corporate venture arm (Canadia Group) rather than international or purely financial venture capital, tapping strategic capital with deep local market relationships and potential operational synergies.
- Became the flagship "first investment" for a newly launched fund, a position that typically comes with elevated visibility and strategic support as the fund's manager builds their own track record around the deal's success.
- Operated within a Cambodian delivery market context recently validated by a major acquisition (Grab buying Nham24 in 2024), suggesting Jalat benefited from broader investor confidence in Cambodia's e-commerce and logistics infrastructure buildout.

## Why it matters (pattern / lesson)

Jalat Logistics demonstrates how founder-led last-mile logistics startups in smaller Southeast Asian markets can attract strategic capital from domestic conglomerates launching their own corporate venture arms — a funding source distinct from and complementary to traditional international VC, often bringing deeper local market access and operational relationships. Being selected as a new fund's debut investment is also a notable pattern worth recognizing: such deals often carry outsized strategic support from the fund manager, since the fund's own credibility becomes partly tied to that first portfolio company's success.
