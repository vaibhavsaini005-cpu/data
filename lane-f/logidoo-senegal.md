## One-liner

Logidoo, a Senegalese logistics platform founded by Tamsir Ousmane Traore operating across eight Francophone North and West African markets, secured investment from Mercy Corps Ventures in June 2026 to strengthen cross-border logistics corridors connecting Senegal, Côte d'Ivoire, Morocco, Mali, Mauritania, Guinea, The Gambia, and Tunisia.

## What happened

Logidoo built a digital logistics platform addressing a structural gap in Francophone North and West Africa: businesses moving goods across the region's numerous international borders previously had to coordinate fragmented, often informal logistics arrangements corridor by corridor. Logidoo's platform suite covers the full logistics value chain — a freight marketplace matching cross-border shipment demand with carrier capacity, fulfilment and merchant ERP integration, a last-mile delivery network within Senegal, a heavy freight and trucking aggregator in Côte d'Ivoire, and a cross-border marketplace connecting African producers directly to regional and diaspora buyers.

Mercy Corps Ventures, the venture arm of the global humanitarian and development organization Mercy Corps, invested in Logidoo specifically to help strengthen logistics corridors across Francophone Africa — a strategic focus reflecting Mercy Corps Ventures' broader mandate of backing startups that improve economic resilience and market access in underserved regions. The investment followed an earlier 2024 funding round in which Logidoo had raised $1.5 million from other investors, showing a company that had already demonstrated enough traction across its multi-country corridor network to attract a second, strategically aligned round from an impact-oriented venture investor.

## Key decisions / turning points

- Built a full-stack logistics platform spanning freight marketplace, fulfilment/ERP integration, last-mile delivery, and heavy-freight aggregation rather than a single narrow logistics product, positioning the company to capture value across the entire regional shipping value chain.
- Expanded operations across eight distinct Francophone North and West African markets simultaneously (Senegal, Côte d'Ivoire, Morocco, Mali, Mauritania, Guinea, The Gambia, Tunisia) rather than deepening a single-country position first, betting on regional network effects across borders.
- Attracted a development-and-impact-oriented investor (Mercy Corps Ventures) specifically focused on strengthening cross-border logistics corridors, aligning investor mission with the company's core value proposition of reducing regional trade friction.
- Built on an earlier 2024 funding round rather than requiring this to be a first institutional raise, demonstrating a sequential capital-raising pattern tied to expanding operational traction across its multi-country network.

## Why it matters (pattern / lesson)

Logidoo demonstrates how logistics platforms addressing genuine cross-border trade friction in Francophone Africa can attract impact-oriented venture capital specifically because their business model directly serves development goals — reducing shipping costs and improving market access across a region with historically fragmented, border-heavy logistics infrastructure. The company's simultaneous multi-country expansion across eight markets, rather than a single-market-first approach, also illustrates how cross-border logistics businesses in regions with strong regional trade agreements (like AfCFTA) may need to build multi-country network coverage from a relatively early stage, since the core value of a freight marketplace depends on liquidity across the corridors it connects rather than depth within any single national market alone.
