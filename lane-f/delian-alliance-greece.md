## One-liner

Athens-based Delian Alliance Industries, founded by former Apple autonomous-vehicle roboticist Dimitrios Kottas, raised a $14M Series A in July 2025 to build autonomous surveillance towers, counter-drone systems, and electronic warfare technology for Europe's borders.

## What happened

Delian Alliance Industries was founded in 2021 as Lambda Automata by Dimitrios Kottas, who spent five years at Apple working on autonomous car technology within its Special Projects Group before pivoting to defense technology and basing the company in Greece, partly to take advantage of lower operational costs than Western European or US defense-tech hubs. The company builds AI-powered autonomous surveillance towers with advanced cameras and software, alongside electronic warfare systems, loitering munitions, and GPS-denied navigation technology aimed at counter-drone and area-denial operations.

The July 2025 $14M Series A was co-led by Air Street Capital and Marathon Venture Capital, with participation from 201 Ventures, HCVC, Entropy Industrial Capital, and Nebular — a syndicate blending specialist deep-tech and defense-focused funds with regional European venture capital. The funding supports expansion of the company's autonomous defense systems across Europe's borders, positioning Delian Alliance within the broader European push to build domestic defense-technology capacity amid heightened regional security concerns.

## Key decisions / turning points

- Relocated/founded operations in Greece specifically to access lower operational costs relative to Western European defense-tech hubs, while still serving the broader European defense market.
- Brought Silicon Valley autonomous-systems engineering expertise (five years on Apple's autonomous vehicle Special Projects Group) directly into a defense-hardware category, cross-applying consumer-tech-grade autonomy engineering to military and border-security applications.
- Built a product portfolio spanning surveillance towers, electronic warfare, and loitering munitions rather than a single product line, positioning the company as a broader autonomous-defense-systems platform rather than a narrow point solution.
- Attracted a funding syndicate combining specialist defense/deep-tech investors (Air Street Capital) with regional venture funds (Marathon Venture Capital), signaling both sector-specific and geography-specific investor confidence.

## Why it matters (pattern / lesson)

Delian Alliance's raise illustrates how European defense-tech startups are increasingly able to attract meaningful venture capital by combining Silicon Valley-caliber autonomous-systems engineering talent with lower-cost European operating bases — a geographic arbitrage that widens margins while still serving the same high-value European defense market. The founder's direct pivot from consumer autonomous-vehicle technology (Apple) into military autonomy also reflects a broader talent-flow pattern: engineering expertise built in commercial autonomy is increasingly transferable into, and valued within, the defense-technology sector.
