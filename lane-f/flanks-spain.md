## One-liner

Flanks, a Barcelona-based wealth management automation platform used by over 100 banks and advisors to manage more than $39 billion in assets, raised a €14M round led by Motive Ventures and Battery Ventures in February 2025 to expand its Flanks LUME platform across Europe.

## What happened

Flanks, founded in 2019 by Joaquim de la Cruz (a Forbes 30 Under 30 European Finance honoree), Álvaro Morales (former Global Head of Santander Private Banking), and software engineer Sergi Lao, built Flanks LUME — an end-to-end platform that aggregates, reconciles, and enriches wealth management data across all asset classes from both public and private markets globally. The platform automates workflows that traditionally required manual reconciliation across fragmented data sources, providing banks and advisors with 360-degree client reporting, portfolio insights, and custom projections.

The February 2025 €14M round, led by Motive Ventures and Battery Ventures with continued participation from existing investors Earlybird, JME Ventures, and 4Founders Capital, builds on a founding team combining fintech entrepreneurship experience with deep private banking industry credibility (Morales's prior role leading Santander Private Banking). By the time of the raise, Flanks had already achieved substantial enterprise traction — over 100 banks, advisors, and large financial institutions using the platform to manage portfolios collectively worth more than $39 billion. The capital is earmarked for accelerating European growth and developing new capabilities including order execution and automated investment proposals.

## Key decisions / turning points

- Combined a founding team with both fintech-building experience and deep incumbent private-banking credibility (a former Santander Private Banking global head), giving the company insider understanding of the specific reconciliation and reporting pain points wealth managers face.
- Built infrastructure aggregating data across all asset classes and both public and private markets, rather than specializing narrowly in one asset type, positioning Flanks as comprehensive wealth-data infrastructure rather than a point solution.
- Achieved substantial enterprise scale (100+ institutions, $39B+ in managed portfolios) before this funding round, using demonstrated commercial traction with major financial institutions as the primary evidence supporting the raise.
- Planned expansion into adjacent capabilities (order execution, automated investment proposals) building outward from its core data-aggregation strength, rather than pursuing an entirely separate product line.

## Why it matters (pattern / lesson)

Flanks demonstrates that fintech infrastructure targeting incumbent wealth managers' operational pain points (fragmented data reconciliation across asset classes) can achieve substantial enterprise adoption when founders combine genuine industry insider credibility with technical execution — the former Santander Private Banking executive's involvement likely accelerated trust-building with the exact institutional customer base the product targets. Building broad, comprehensive data-aggregation infrastructure (all asset classes, public and private markets) rather than a narrow point solution also positions such platforms to expand into adjacent high-value capabilities (like order execution) once the core data layer achieves scale, rather than requiring an entirely new product for expansion.
