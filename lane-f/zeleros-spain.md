## One-liner

Zeleros CEO and co-founder David Pistoni filed for voluntary insolvency in March 2026 after a restructuring failed to reach break-even; Amper bought the Spanish company's assets for about €1 million in May and took on 18 employees.

## What happened

Zeleros raised more than €15 million to develop hyperloop technology, then tried to commercialize adjacent electrification, energy-storage, and advanced-mobility systems. The shift increased annual revenue by 260%, from €500,000 to €1.8 million in 2024, but it did not produce financial self-sufficiency. A 2023 restructuring had already cut almost half the original workforce.

Pistoni said management did not want to prolong the company's financial distress after additional capital still failed to bring it to break-even. Zeleros filed for voluntary insolvency on March 16, 2026, and a Valencia commercial court declared the proceeding on March 30 for liquidation. On May 25, Amper confirmed that it had completed the acquisition of Zeleros' assets and technological capabilities for approximately €1 million, integrating 18 employees under Pistoni.

## Key decisions / turning points

- Management shifted from a capital-intensive hyperloop vision toward nearer-term electrification, battery, and advanced-mobility applications.
- The pivot lifted revenue to €1.8 million in 2024 but did not solve the company's funding and break-even problem.
- Pistoni and the company chose voluntary insolvency in March 2026 instead of extending an unsustainable cash position.
- The court-supervised process ended the company as an independent venture while transferring its technology and 18 jobs to Amper for about €1 million.

## Why it matters (pattern / lesson)

Zeleros is a resolved example of a technically productive pivot that arrived too late to repair the capital structure. Revenue growth alone was insufficient: after more than €15 million raised, the independent company ended in liquidation and its assets sold for roughly €1 million. Deep-tech founders must match commercialization timelines to financing capacity, not only demonstrate that adjacent markets can generate sales.
