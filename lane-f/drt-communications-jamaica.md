## One-liner

One Great Studio CEO and co-founder Djuvane Browne acquired Danielle Terrelonge's DRT Communications for J$115 million in February 2025; after integration, DRT remained an operating brand as the group reached J$212 million in first-half 2026 revenue and J$24 million in net profit.

## What happened

Terrelonge built DRT Communications into a Jamaican public-relations, strategic-communications, and media-intelligence business. One Great Studio CEO and co-founder Djuvane Browne wanted to broaden his listed digital agency into a portfolio of specialist brands, and identified DRT as the public-relations and media-monitoring component of that strategy.

The acquisition closed on February 18, 2025 for total consideration of J$115 million, including a J$35 million performance-based earnout over three years. One Great Studio then integrated DRT into shared finance, human-resources, technology, and reporting systems. The combined group absorbed J$25 million in 2025 losses partly associated with integration and other investments, but by the first half of 2026 it reported J$212 million in revenue, up 17% year over year, and J$24 million in net profit versus a J$15 million loss in the prior-year period. DRT remained a named operating brand in the portfolio.

## Key decisions / turning points

- Browne chose to acquire DRT as the public-relations and media-intelligence component of a listed regional agency group.
- Browne structured the J$115 million purchase with J$35 million contingent on performance over three years, aligning part of the consideration with post-deal results.
- One Great Studio retained DRT as a specialist brand while centralizing back-office services rather than erasing it in a full brand consolidation.
- The group accepted near-term integration costs, then returned to profitability in 2026 while continuing to operate DRT inside the portfolio.

## Why it matters (pattern / lesson)

DRT provides a resolved acquisition-and-integration case. The sale had a disclosed price and performance mechanism, the acquired brand remained visible, and the buyer's subsequent financial results show that the wider portfolio moved from loss to profit after the integration period. For founder-led service firms, joining a shared operating platform can preserve specialist positioning while adding regional distribution and back-office scale.
