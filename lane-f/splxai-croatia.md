## One-liner

SplxAI, a Croatian-founded AI security startup founded in 2023 by Kristian Kamber and Ante Gojsalic, closed a $7M seed round in March 2025 and was acquired by cybersecurity giant Zscaler in November 2025 as part of a combined $692 million acquisition alongside Red Canary Inc.

## What happened

SplxAI built a platform helping organizations discover, test, and protect their AI models, agentic workflows, and underlying AI infrastructure through automated security testing, dynamic remediation, and continuous monitoring — addressing the emerging security gap created by enterprises rapidly deploying internal and customer-facing AI agents without corresponding security tooling built for that new attack surface. The company's platform specifically targeted AI-native risks like prompt injection, model manipulation, and agent misuse that traditional cybersecurity tools weren't designed to catch.

The company's March 2025 $7M ($6.5M) seed round, led by Bulgaria's LAUNCHub Ventures with participation from Rain Capital, Inovo, Runtime Ventures, DNV Ventures, and South Central Ventures, was — at the time — reported as the largest investment in a Croatian startup in the previous two years. Just eight months later, on November 3, 2025, Zscaler announced its acquisition of SplxAI as part of a combined $692 million transaction alongside Red Canary Inc., explicitly framed as strengthening Zscaler's ability to secure the "enterprise AI lifecycle" — AI asset discovery, automated red-teaming, and governance — within its broader zero-trust security platform.

## Key decisions / turning points

- Built specifically for AI-native security risks (prompt injection, agent misuse, model manipulation) rather than adapting traditional application-security tooling, positioning the company squarely in the security category enterprises were most urgently trying to address as AI agent adoption accelerated.
- Achieved a rapid seed-to-acquisition timeline — roughly eight months from closing the seed round to being acquired — reflecting how quickly strategic acquirers were moving to secure AI-security capability amid the broader enterprise AI adoption wave.
- Attracted a Southeast European-led investor syndicate (Bulgaria's LAUNCHub Ventures as lead) rather than relying solely on Western European or US investors, demonstrating that regional Balkan/CEE VC firms could successfully back and exit a category-leading AI-security startup.
- Was acquired as part of a combined transaction alongside Red Canary rather than as a standalone deal, suggesting Zscaler viewed the two acquisitions as complementary pieces of a broader AI-security and detection capability build-out.

## Why it matters (pattern / lesson)

SplxAI's rapid seed-to-exit trajectory demonstrates how urgently large cybersecurity platforms were racing to acquire AI-native security capability in 2025, as enterprise AI agent adoption created attack surfaces that traditional security tooling wasn't built to address — a strategic acquirer's willingness to buy rather than build signals how nascent and fast-moving this security category was. The deal also reinforces that Southeast European tech ecosystems (Croatia, with Bulgarian lead investment) can produce and successfully exit category-defining AI-security startups, competing directly for strategic acquirer attention against companies from more established, larger startup hubs.
