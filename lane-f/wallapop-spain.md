## One-liner

CEO Rob Cassedy agreed on August 5, 2025 to sell Wallapop's remaining 70.5% to NAVER for €377 million; after the January 2026 close, the Spanish marketplace contributed KRW87.6 billion of first-half revenue and KRW5.6 billion of net profit to its buyer.

## What happened

NAVER had invested €190 million in Wallapop since 2021 and owned 29.5%, but both companies concluded that deeper integration was needed. Cassedy endorsed NAVER as the partner that could bring search, advertising, payments and AI technology into Wallapop, and on August 5, 2025 they announced the sale of the remaining stake.

The transaction closed on January 30, 2026 for €377 million at a €600 million equity valuation, with Cassedy, the Barcelona headquarters and roughly 300 employees retained. By August, Wallapop had contributed KRW87.6 billion in revenue and KRW5.6 billion in net profit to NAVER's first-half results. Wallapop later reported 2025 revenue of €116 million, up 15%, and more than 23 million monthly users.

## Key decisions / turning points

- On August 5, 2025, Cassedy and Wallapop's board chose full strategic ownership by NAVER after several years of minority investment and technology collaboration.
- NAVER paid €377 million for the final 70.5%; the January close valued Wallapop at €600 million before cash, below its €806 million 2024 funding valuation.
- The buyer retained Wallapop's leadership, Barcelona base and 300-person workforce rather than folding the marketplace into another brand.
- In the first half of 2026, Wallapop was profitable in NAVER's consolidated accounts, contributing KRW5.6 billion of net profit on KRW87.6 billion of revenue.

## Why it matters (pattern / lesson)

A lower headline valuation can still produce a resolved strategic outcome. Wallapop traded valuation prestige for full access to a proven C2C owner's technology and balance sheet; the completed close, employee incentive payout and immediate positive consolidated profit show tangible results beyond the announcement.
