## One-liner

Founded in 2014, Korean home-interior platform Ohouse (operated by Bucketplace) became a unicorn in May 2022 after raising a $182 million Series D at a $1.6 billion valuation, turning a home-design content app into a full-stack interior commerce and services super-app.

## What happened

Bucketplace launched Ohouse as an app for users to browse and share real-life home interior photos and ideas. Over time it layered on an e-commerce marketplace for furniture and home goods, remodeling and repair services, professional-matching for interior work, and moving services — becoming what investors described as a "lifestyle super-app" rather than a single-purpose content or shopping app. The company raised a $77 million Series C in 2020, putting it on a path toward unicorn status, and followed with a $182 million Series D announced May 8-9, 2022, led by SoftBank Ventures Asia and Vertex Growth (anchored by Temasek-backed Vertex Holdings), with participation from BRV Capital Management, BOND, Korea Development Bank, IMM Investment, and Mirae Asset Capital. The round valued Bucketplace at roughly $1.6 billion, making it one of South Korea's newest unicorns at the time, and the company said the capital would fund AR-powered design tools and international expansion.

## Key decisions / turning points

- **Content-to-commerce sequencing**: building the user base around home-design inspiration content first, then monetizing via an integrated marketplace and services layer once engagement was established, rather than launching as a pure e-commerce play.
- **Bundling adjacent services (remodeling, repair, moving) into one app**: this expanded revenue per user well beyond a single furniture-sale transaction and made the platform stickier across a customer's entire home-improvement journey.
- **Raising from strategic and sovereign-linked capital (SoftBank Ventures Asia, Temasek-linked Vertex, Korea Development Bank)**: this combination signaled durability to the market at a moment (mid-2022) when many growth-stage tech valuations were already under pressure globally.

## Why it matters (pattern / lesson)

Ohouse is a case of a vertical content community successfully expanding into a full-stack commerce and services super-app in a single national market before international capital arrived at scale. Its unicorn round closed in May 2022, just as the broader Series D/growth-equity market for Asian consumer tech was cooling — evidence that a platform with real engagement and a proven bundled-monetization model could still raise nine-figure rounds even as the funding environment tightened elsewhere.
