## One-liner

Techcoop, a Ho Chi Minh City-headquartered agritech platform providing traceability, financing, and market access for export-oriented agricultural supply chains, closed a $70 million Series A round in February 2025 — combining $28 million in equity and $42 million in debt — co-led by TNB Aura and AVV, in one of Southeast Asia's largest agritech Series A rounds to date.

## What happened

Techcoop built a B2B platform addressing structural gaps in Vietnam's export-oriented agricultural supply chains, providing traceability tools, flexible financing, and market access services to help agricultural producers meet the documentation and financing requirements of international buyers. This combination directly addresses a persistent bottleneck for smallholder and mid-sized agricultural exporters: limited access to working capital and the compliance/traceability infrastructure increasingly demanded by export markets, particularly in premium agricultural sectors.

The $70 million Series A round, closed in February 2025, notably combined $28 million in equity financing with $42 million in debt financing — a structure reflecting Techcoop's dual role as both a technology platform and, through its financing arm, a capital provider to its agricultural supply-chain participants. The round was co-led by TNB Aura and AVV, with additional participation from BlueOrchard, FMO, AppWorks, and Capria Ventures — a syndicate spanning impact-oriented development finance institutions alongside traditional venture capital, reflecting the dual commercial and development impact positioning of Techcoop's agricultural financing model.

## Key decisions / turning points

- Structured its Series A specifically as a blend of equity and debt capital ($28M/$42M), aligning the debt component directly with Techcoop's core business of extending financing to agricultural supply-chain participants.
- Focused on export-oriented agricultural supply chains specifically, rather than the broader domestic agricultural market, targeting a segment with clearer compliance and traceability requirements that its platform could directly address.
- Attracted a syndicate combining impact-focused development finance institutions (BlueOrchard, FMO) with traditional venture capital (TNB Aura, AVV, AppWorks, Capria Ventures), reflecting the company's positioning at the intersection of commercial agritech and financial inclusion.
- Built traceability infrastructure as a core product component rather than an add-on, addressing increasingly stringent international buyer requirements for supply-chain transparency in agricultural exports.

## Why it matters (pattern / lesson)

Techcoop's landmark Series A demonstrates how agritech platforms addressing export-market compliance requirements — rather than purely domestic agricultural productivity — can access substantial blended debt-and-equity financing, since the traceability and financing services such platforms provide map directly onto measurable trade-facilitation and risk-reduction outcomes that attract both commercial and development-finance investors simultaneously. The scale of the round, one of the largest agritech Series A raises in Southeast Asia, also signals growing investor conviction that agricultural supply-chain infrastructure — long an underinvested category relative to consumer-facing fintech and e-commerce — can support venture-scale outcomes when tied to concrete export revenue and financing needs.
