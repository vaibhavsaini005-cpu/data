## One-liner

Great Deals grew from a small Filipino distributor into the country's largest e-commerce enabler by running storefronts, fulfilment, and digital marketing for 250+ global brands on Shopee, Lazada, and TikTok Shop — scaling through two funding rounds rather than chasing a consumer-facing exit.

## What happened

Founded by Steve Sy in 2014, Great Deals took a "picks and shovels" position in Philippine e-commerce: instead of building its own marketplace, it became a full-service enabler running end-to-end operations — storefront management, digital marketing, content creation, web design, business analytics, warehousing, and peak-scaling fulfilment — on behalf of brands selling through Shopee, Lazada, and other platforms.

The company raised ₱600M (~$12M) from private equity firm Navegar in a round closing January 23, 2020, earmarked for IT infrastructure, warehouse capacity, and technology to support aggressive expansion. Growth continued through the pandemic e-commerce boom, and in May 2021 Great Deals closed a $30M Series B from Fast Group, CVC Capital, and returning investor Navegar, used in part to build an automated fulfilment center in the Philippines. By 2023–2024 the company was serving major clients including Nestlé, P&G, NutriAsia, Del Monte, Unilever, and Maybelline, was named among the Philippines' Top 1000 Corporations, and reported roughly ₱12.4B in 2024 revenue, with stated ambitions to scale to ₱100B by 2030 and expand across the Asia-Pacific region.

## Key decisions / turning points

- **Enabler, not marketplace**: rather than competing head-on with Shopee/Lazada for consumer attention, Great Deals positioned itself as critical infrastructure for the brands selling on those platforms — lower platform risk, recurring B2B revenue.
- **Sequenced, purpose-tied fundraising**: the 2020 round funded infrastructure ahead of demand; the 2021 round funded automated fulfilment — each raise tied to a concrete operational bottleneck rather than general runway.
- **Riding platform growth without platform dependency risk**: by servicing brands across multiple marketplaces (Shopee, Lazada, TikTok Shop) rather than exclusively one, Great Deals avoided being wiped out by any single platform's policy or fee changes.

## Why it matters (pattern / lesson)

Great Deals is a clean example of the "e-commerce enabler" model that has produced regional successes across Southeast Asia (e.g. aCommerce, SEA's broader enabler wave) — capturing value from marketplace growth without the customer-acquisition cost of building a consumer brand. Its funding pattern (small infrastructure round, then a scaling round tied to automation) is a reusable template for operationally-intensive B2B businesses in markets where e-commerce logistics infrastructure is still maturing.
