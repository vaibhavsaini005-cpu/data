## One-liner

Bangkok-based e-commerce enabler Crea, co-founded and co-run by Aimone Ripa Di Meana and Alessandro Piscini, sold a 51% stake to Vietnam's OnPoint in December 2024, folding Thailand's leading e-commerce enablement operation into a regional group serving 250+ brands.

## What happened

Aimone Ripa Di Meana and Alessandro Piscini, both e-commerce veterans, co-founded Crea in Thailand in 2019 as an "e-commerce enabler" — running fulfillment, marketplace management, livestreaming and brand operations for consumer brands selling online, a category that had become a critical layer of Southeast Asia's e-commerce infrastructure. Crea grew into one of Thailand's leading players in that space.

On December 16, 2024 (announced December 18), Vietnam-based e-commerce enabler OnPoint — led by founder and CEO Trần Vũ Quang, and backed by a prior $50 million investment from Temasek-linked SeaTown — acquired a 51% majority stake in Crea. The two companies framed the deal as a merger of regional capabilities rather than a straightforward buyout: OnPoint's Vietnam-honed social-commerce and livestreaming expertise combined with Crea's Thailand market position, creating a combined operation serving more than 250 brands with over 600 in-house staff and 1,000+ outsourced personnel across warehousing, livestreaming and operations, aimed at Southeast Asia's e-commerce market projected to reach $370 billion by 2030.

## Key decisions / turning points

- **Selling majority control (51%) rather than staying independent or raising further private funding**: Ripa Di Meana and Piscini chose to fold Crea into a larger regional platform instead of continuing to compete as a standalone Thai enabler.
- **OnPoint's decision to expand into Thailand via acquisition rather than organic entry**: Trần Vũ Quang used OnPoint's SeaTown-backed capital to buy an established local leader instead of building Thai market share from zero.
- **Framing the deal as a capability merger, not an exit**: both founding teams stayed on to run the combined Thailand-Vietnam operation, betting that scale across two of Southeast Asia's largest e-commerce markets mattered more than remaining a single-country specialist.

## Why it matters (pattern / lesson)

Crea is a clean example of consolidation in Southeast Asia's e-commerce-enablement layer: rather than each country's leading enabler independently building toward the same regional scale, capital-backed players (OnPoint, fueled by Temasek-linked funding) are acquiring country champions to assemble a multi-market platform faster than organic expansion would allow. For founders in adjacent, still-fragmented markets, it's a signal that being the best local operator in a fast-consolidating vertical is often more valuable as an acquisition target than as an independent fundraising story.
