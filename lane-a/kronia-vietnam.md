## One-liner

After his B2B e-commerce startup Telio collapsed in late 2024, founder Bui Sy Phong pivoted entirely out of fintech/e-commerce into gaming, launching studio Kronia with a $2.5 million development-support deal from VNG — the same investor that had lost its money in Telio.

## What happened

Bui Sy Phong had already founded one fintech company, OnOnPay, in 2014, before starting Telio, a B2B e-commerce startup that connected traditional mom-and-pop retailers in Vietnam to suppliers and had raised backing from Tiger Global and Granite Asia. Telio collapsed in late 2024, wiping out investor capital including VNG's stake.

Rather than cut ties, VNG — a Vietnamese tech conglomerate best known as a game publisher — chose to back Phong's next venture: Kronia, a game studio focused on premium PC and console titles for a global audience. The deal, reported by DealStreetAsia on July 21, 2025 and detailed further by Tech in Asia on September 16, 2025, structures VNG's support as a $2.5 million co-development package delivered through engineers, artists, and infrastructure rather than a straight cash check. Kronia's debut project, codenamed "Project P," carries an ambitious $10-12 million production budget, a targeted 2029 launch, and a goal of 4 million copies sold for roughly $200 million in revenue. Unusually for Vietnam's startup ecosystem, Phong retains full creative control and IP ownership of the studio, with VNG positioned to make additional equity investment tied to future performance rather than owning the IP outright.

## Key decisions / turning points

- **Walking away from fintech/e-commerce entirely after Telio's failure**, rather than attempting a same-sector relaunch, betting instead on a category (premium global gaming) Vietnam has rarely attempted at this budget level.
- **VNG re-investing in the same founder who had just lost its prior investment**, treating founder track record and domain-agnostic execution ability as worth backing again rather than writing off the relationship after a failure.
- **Structuring the deal as development-in-kind plus founder-retained IP**, instead of a standard equity check — VNG evolves from game *publisher* toward original-IP creator while Phong avoids ceding control the way he may have in his prior ventures.

## Why it matters (pattern / lesson)

Kronia is a rare, well-documented case of an investor doubling down on a founder immediately after a portfolio failure, rather than the more common Vietnamese-ecosystem pattern of the same founder quietly relaunching without local backers' involvement. It also shows an incumbent (VNG, a publisher) using founder-pivot funding as a structured route into original global IP — a strategic bet on people and category-timing over the safety of a founder's prior sector experience.
