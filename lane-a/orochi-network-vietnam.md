## One-liner

Vietnamese cryptography startup Orochi Network, led by CEO Kieu Diem Tran, raised a $12 million seed round to build zero-knowledge-proof-based "verifiable data infrastructure" meant to replace traditional blockchain oracles.

## What happened

Founded in 2021, Orochi Network built technology using Zero-Knowledge Proofs to let Web3 applications verify the accuracy of off-chain data without exposing the underlying private data — an alternative to traditional blockchain oracles, which typically require trusting a third party to relay external data on-chain. By 2025 the company's infrastructure was in use across more than 40 blockchain platforms, with enterprise customers and grant support from the Ethereum Foundation, Mina Protocol, Web3 Foundation and BNB Chain.

On March 10, 2025, Orochi Network announced a $12 million seed round from investors including Presto Labs, Smape Capital, Anti Capital, Maxx Capital, Bolts Capital, AZA Ventures and Connectico. CEO Kieu Diem Tran framed the raise as validation of the company's core technical bet: "Securing this funding validates our vision for data integrity. The investment will allow us to expand infrastructure, enter new markets, and build strategic partnerships."

## Key decisions / turning points

- **Building on Zero-Knowledge Proofs to solve data verification rather than adopting the standard oracle model**: a specific technical differentiation from incumbent blockchain infrastructure (traditional oracles) that Tran and the team bet enterprise and Web3 customers would value enough to switch to.
- **Securing grants from major blockchain foundations (Ethereum Foundation, Web3 Foundation, BNB Chain, Mina Protocol) before the seed raise**: used foundation-level technical validation as a credibility signal ahead of institutional VC funding.
- **Raising from crypto-native funds (Presto Labs, Smape Capital, etc.) specifically to fund infrastructure expansion and new-market entry**, rather than raising opportunistically on hype alone — Tran ties the capital directly to those two stated uses.

## Why it matters (pattern / lesson)

Orochi Network's path illustrates a credibility-building sequence available to deep-tech Web3 startups from smaller ecosystems: win technical validation and grant funding from the major protocol foundations that define a niche (here, verifiable data/oracles) before raising institutional capital, using that foundation backing as proof of technical merit rather than relying on venture brand names alone. It also shows a Vietnamese blockchain infrastructure company competing directly on a global technical thesis (replacing oracles with ZK-based verification) rather than building region-specific consumer crypto products.
