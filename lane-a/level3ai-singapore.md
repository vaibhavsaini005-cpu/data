## One-liner

Singapore's Level3AI, co-founded by CEO Harry Yu and Zachary Wang, raised a $13 million seed round led by Lightspeed after bootstrapping profitably for 18 months on a model that refunds enterprise customers if its AI agents miss agreed performance benchmarks.

## What happened

Harry Yu and Zachary Wang founded Level3AI in July 2024 to build enterprise-grade AI agents that manage end-to-end customer engagement across voice, email and chat, integrating large language models with clients' existing CRM, telephony and ticketing systems. Rather than selling AI agents as a generic tool, the company built its go-to-market model around guaranteeing specific outcomes — customer satisfaction scores or conversion rates — with full refunds if agreed benchmarks were not met, and tuned its platform for APAC's linguistic and cultural diversity (for example, gender-appropriate forms of address in Malaysia and multilingual code-switching in markets like Hong Kong).

The company bootstrapped profitably for 18 months, powering more than 10 million interactions for over 1 million end consumers for brands including GetGo, Carousell, Yuu Rewards and Carsome, before raising a $13 million seed round led by Lightspeed Venture Partners, with BEENEXT, 500 Global, Sovereign's Capital and Goodwater Capital also participating (announced January 22, 2026). Yu tied the raise to a specific technological inflection point: "Recent advancements in AI have created an opportunity to improve customer experience at scale," stating the company would focus on delivering measurable outcomes for enterprise customers, with the capital directed at R&D and expanding Level3AI's operational footprint across Asia-Pacific.

## Key decisions / turning points

- **Building a refund-backed, outcome-guaranteed pricing model instead of a standard SaaS subscription**: Yu and Wang made the AI agents' performance contractually measurable (CSAT, conversion) rather than asking enterprise buyers to trust an unverified AI product.
- **Staying bootstrapped and profitable for 18 months before raising outside capital**: used real enterprise deployments across four named APAC brands as proof of the model before seeking a seed round, rather than raising pre-revenue.
- **Localizing the product specifically for APAC linguistic and cultural variation** (e.g., Malaysia's gender-appropriate address forms, Hong Kong code-switching) rather than deploying a single English-language model region-wide.

## Why it matters (pattern / lesson)

Level3AI's raise shows a credible playbook for enterprise AI startups navigating buyer skepticism about AI product quality: convert the trust problem into a commercial guarantee (refund if benchmarks are missed) and prove it with real paying customers before raising venture capital, rather than raising on a demo or roadmap. Bootstrapping profitably to real usage scale (10M+ interactions, 1M+ end consumers) before a $13M seed also inverts the more common regional pattern of raising early and proving the model with investor capital — a signal of founder conviction that outcome-guarantees, not funding, were the binding constraint on enterprise adoption.
