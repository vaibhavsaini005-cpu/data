## One-liner

Base.vn, a Vietnamese SaaS platform for hiring, payroll and team collaboration founded in 2016, was acquired for a majority stake by tech conglomerate FPT Corporation in May 2021, one of the clearest homegrown-SaaS exit stories in Vietnam's startup scene.

## What happened

Base Enterprise (Base.vn) was founded in August 2016 by Pham Kim Hung, building a suite of SaaS tools covering recruitment, performance management, payroll and internal workflow automation aimed at Vietnamese small and mid-sized businesses. By 2020-2021 the platform served more than 5,000 corporate clients in Vietnam and, in December 2020, won first prize for excellent digital platform in Vietnam's "Make in Vietnam" digital product awards, run by the Ministry of Information and Communications.

On 4 May 2021, FPT Corporation — Vietnam's largest listed technology company — announced it had acquired a majority (controlling) stake in Base.vn, buying out the startup's existing venture-capital investors in the process. Deal terms were not publicly disclosed. The companies framed the tie-up as combining Base.vn's SaaS product with FPT's cloud, AI and blockchain infrastructure to serve a stated addressable market of 800,000 Vietnamese enterprises undergoing digital transformation. Hung remained CEO of Base.vn following the deal, continuing to operate it as a distinct product line inside the FPT group; in 2023 coverage noted the unit had separately drawn in further external capital (a $3.5M raise) even after majority FPT ownership.

## Key decisions / turning points

- **Focused on unglamorous back-office SaaS rather than consumer apps**: Base.vn built recruitment/HR/workflow tooling for Vietnamese SMEs at a time when much later-stage local VC attention chased consumer marketplaces and delivery.
- **Government recognition (Dec 2020) preceded the acquisition**: winning a national "Make in Vietnam" award raised Base.vn's profile with strategic acquirers just months before the FPT deal closed.
- **FPT bought out VCs rather than co-investing**: FPT's move to acquire a controlling stake and cash out prior investors, rather than simply lead a funding round, signalled a full strategic acquisition rather than a growth-equity check.
- **Founder retained operational control post-acquisition**: keeping Hung as CEO preserved product continuity instead of folding Base.vn fully into FPT's existing enterprise software lines.

## Why it matters (pattern / lesson)

Base.vn is a rare Vietnamese example of a domestic B2B SaaS company reaching a successful strategic exit to a local conglomerate rather than a foreign acquirer or an IPO — a path more commonly associated with markets like India or Southeast Asia's fintech sector. It illustrates how a large incumbent (FPT) uses bolt-on acquisitions of focused SaaS startups to accelerate its own enterprise digital-transformation pitch, and shows that unglamorous back-office software can be a durable, exitable business in a market still dominated by consumer-tech narratives.
