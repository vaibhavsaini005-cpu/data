## One-liner

Coolmate, a Vietnamese direct-to-consumer menswear brand, closed a Series C funding round led by Vertex Growth Fund on November 3, 2025 — with participation from Cool Japan Fund, YoungOne CVC, and existing backers Vertex Ventures SEA & India and Kairous Capital — to fund international expansion, a push into women's apparel, and offline retail growth through 2030.

## What happened

Coolmate built a direct-to-consumer menswear brand in Vietnam, selling apparel through its own e-commerce channels rather than relying primarily on third-party marketplaces, a model that let it capture higher margins and closer customer relationships than typical Southeast Asian fashion retailers. The company progressed through a methodical sequence of funding rounds since its founding — a $2 million Series A in 2022 backed by Access Ventures, Do Ventures, CyberAgent Capital, and DSG Consumer Partners, followed by an additional Series A extension, a Series B in October 2024, and now this Series C round — building a track record of institutional investor confidence at each successive stage.

The November 2025 Series C round brought in a notably international investor syndicate, including Japan-government-backed Cool Japan Fund and South Korean YoungOne CVC alongside its existing regional investors, and was explicitly framed around three strategic priorities for 2026-2030: "Go Women" (expanding beyond its original menswear focus), "Go Global" (entering new international markets), and "Go Offline" (building physical retail presence to complement its e-commerce-first model). This three-pronged expansion signals a homegrown Vietnamese brand transitioning from a domestic online-only retailer toward a broader, omnichannel, potentially regional consumer brand.

## Key decisions / turning points

- Built the business around a direct-to-consumer model from the outset rather than depending on third-party marketplace distribution, retaining margin and customer-data control as it scaled.
- Progressed through a disciplined sequence of funding rounds (Series A in 2022, extension, Series B in 2024, Series C in 2025) rather than attempting to raise a single large round early, building investor confidence incrementally.
- Attracted international strategic investors (Japan's Cool Japan Fund, South Korea's YoungOne CVC) specifically ahead of its international expansion push, aligning investor expertise with its stated "Go Global" priority.
- Explicitly planned category expansion beyond its founding menswear focus (into womenswear) and channel expansion (into offline retail) as parallel growth vectors rather than pursuing geographic expansion alone.

## Why it matters (pattern / lesson)

Coolmate demonstrates that Vietnamese D2C consumer brands can build sustained, multi-round investor confidence by maintaining a disciplined, incremental fundraising cadence tied to demonstrated growth milestones rather than front-loading large speculative rounds. The company's deliberate three-pronged 2026-2030 strategy — expanding category, geography, and retail channel simultaneously rather than sequentially — also illustrates a pattern among maturing Southeast Asian consumer brands: once a core domestic online model is proven, investors increasingly back parallel expansion across multiple growth vectors at once rather than requiring one axis to fully mature before the next begins.
