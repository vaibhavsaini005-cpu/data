## One-liner

Wongnai, Thailand's leading restaurant review app, merged with LINE MAN (food delivery) in July 2020 with a US$110M investment — pivoting from discovery to delivery and becoming Thailand's dominant food-tech platform.

## What happened

Wongnai launched around 2012 as Thailand's answer to Yelp: a Thai-language restaurant review and discovery app with millions of user-generated reviews. It built one of the largest restaurant databases in the country and attracted a loyal audience of urban food enthusiasts. LINE MAN, separately, was the on-demand delivery service of LINE Corporation — the chat and social platform that dominates daily digital life in Thailand.

On July 30, 2020, LINE Corporation announced that LINE MAN would merge with Wongnai in a deal accompanied by a US$110 million capital injection from BRV Capital Management. The combined entity, LINE MAN Wongnai (LMWN), brought together Wongnai's restaurant data and reviews with LINE MAN's logistics network and LINE's massive user base in Thailand. The merger was structured so that Wongnai's restaurant-graph intelligence became the discovery layer on top of LINE MAN's delivery rails.

Post-merger growth was rapid. In January 2022, LMWN raised US$265 million in a Series B led by GIC (Singapore's sovereign wealth fund) and LINE Corporation — at the time the largest single funding round for a Thai startup. The company expanded into grocery delivery, quick-commerce, and fintech adjacent services. By 2024 it was the clear market leader in Thailand's food delivery sector (ahead of Grab Food, Foodpanda, and others), serving tens of millions of orders monthly. As of May 2024, LMWN was weighing an IPO in Thailand or the US.

## Key decisions / turning points

- **Choosing consolidation over competition**: Rather than competing head-to-head as separate products, Wongnai and LINE MAN chose to merge their complementary assets — data vs. logistics — avoiding a costly attrition war.
- **LINE ecosystem leverage**: LINE's installed base across Thailand gave the combined entity instant distribution at zero marginal cost; the merger was as much a distribution play as a product one.
- **GIC Series B timing**: The US$265M round in January 2022 came when food delivery growth was still elevated post-COVID, allowing LMWN to lock in capital and outspend rivals before the funding environment tightened.

## Why it matters (pattern / lesson)

LINE MAN Wongnai shows a pivot done well: Wongnai's pure review-and-discovery model had limited monetization upside; pivoting into transactional food delivery — enabled by a strategic merger rather than a rebuild — created a defensible position in one of Southeast Asia's most competitive verticals. It is a case study in how data-rich consumer apps can unlock value by merging with a logistics or commerce layer rather than trying to build one organically.
