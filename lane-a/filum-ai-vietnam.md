## One-liner

Filum AI, led by CEO Tran Van Vien (previously co-founder of Base.vn, acquired by FPT in 2021), raised $1 million to build AI Agents for customer experience management, betting on positioning Vietnam as a regional AI hub rather than just a domestic SaaS play.

## What happened

Founded in 2020 by a team of Silicon Valley- and Vietnam-based experts, Filum AI built an AI-powered customer experience management (CXM) platform aimed at helping businesses optimize costs and improve customer interactions. Tran Van Vien, who had previously co-founded Base.vn (an enterprise software company acquired by Vietnamese conglomerate FPT in 2021), took over as Filum AI's CEO, bringing a decade of digital-transformation experience to a second venture.

On March 18, 2025, Filum AI announced a $1 million funding round from Nextrans, VinVentures and TheVentures, along with individual strategic investors including Tran Viet Hung (founder of Got It) and Tran Anh Dung (founder of MOG). Vien tied the raise to a specific product bet — building "AI Agents" rather than conventional CXM software — and a broader regional ambition: "As AI continues to shape the future, this funding will unlock opportunities for financial and technological collaborations, strengthening Vietnam's position as a leading AI hub in the region." He said the funds would go toward R&D, faster product development, and expansion into Southeast Asian markets beyond Vietnam.

## Key decisions / turning points

- **Returning to found a second company (Filum AI) after a successful exit (Base.vn to FPT)**: Vien applied his enterprise-software and digital-transformation experience to a new, AI-native product category rather than staying inside the acquirer.
- **Betting on "AI Agents" specifically, rather than traditional dashboard-based CXM software**: a concrete product-architecture choice tied directly to the funding announcement, not a vague AI positioning.
- **Bringing in operator-investors (founders of Got It and MOG) alongside institutional VCs**: added Vietnamese tech-founder credibility and operating insight to the cap table rather than relying only on fund capital.

## Why it matters (pattern / lesson)

Filum AI shows a repeat-founder pattern in Vietnam's tech ecosystem: an exited founder (Vien, via Base.vn's acquisition by FPT) recycling both capital credibility and domain experience into a new, AI-native venture in an adjacent category (enterprise software to CX-focused AI agents). Framing a modest $1 million raise explicitly around Vietnam's positioning as a regional AI hub — rather than just describing the product — is a specific way founders in smaller ecosystems attach their individual fundraising news to a larger national narrative, which can help attract follow-on regional investor attention.
