## One-liner

Zipmex, a Singapore-incorporated crypto exchange dominant in Thailand, halted withdrawals in July 2022 after $53 million in exposure to collapsed crypto lenders Babel Finance and Celsius, ultimately leading to regulatory suspension and user losses.

## What happened

Founded in 2019 and incorporated in Singapore, Zipmex built its largest user base in Thailand, also operating in Indonesia, Singapore, and Australia. It raised Series B funding at a $400 million valuation with Coinbase making a strategic investment.

On 20 July 2022, Zipmex abruptly halted customer withdrawals, citing "volatile market conditions and the resulting financial difficulties of our key business partners." The root cause was $48 million in exposure to Babel Finance (which had itself frozen withdrawals) and $5 million with Celsius Network, both collateral damage from the May 2022 Terra/LUNA collapse.

Zipmex filed for creditor protection (debt moratorium) in Singapore's courts in August 2022 while attempting to raise fresh capital. The Thai SEC filed a police report against the company in September 2022. After years of failed restructuring attempts and regulatory battles, the Thai SEC ordered Zipmex to fully suspend digital asset trading and brokerage activities in Thailand in February 2024.

## Key decisions / turning points

- **High-yield "ZipUp+" product.** Zipmex had attracted retail deposits through a high-yield savings product that routed funds into third-party lending protocols — an opaque risk that users did not understand and that created direct exposure to Babel and Celsius.
- **Concentrated counterparty exposure.** Rather than diversifying treasury across platforms, Zipmex concentrated tens of millions of dollars with two entities that were simultaneously under stress from the same macro event.
- **Regulatory arbitrage backfired.** Operating across multiple Southeast Asian jurisdictions with minimal disclosure requirements meant there was no early-warning enforcement mechanism until users were already locked out.

## Why it matters (pattern / lesson)

Zipmex is a clean case study in how retail crypto exchanges in emerging markets amplified 2022 contagion by acting as unregulated shadow banks. The Thailand/Southeast Asia region's lighter regulatory touch that helped Zipmex grow quickly also meant there was nothing to stop it from taking on fatal counterparty risk. The multi-year regulatory saga afterward shows how difficult and slow redress is for retail investors in cross-border crypto failures.
