## One-liner

Indonesian online furniture marketplace that raised $19 M and scaled to 430 employees, then collapsed in 2022 after a stalled Series D left employees unpaid for months before a Jakarta court formally declared bankruptcy.

## What happened

Fabelio was founded in 2014 to sell and deliver furniture and home-décor products across Indonesian cities. It scaled aggressively: three offices, 20 physical showrooms, and 430 staff. The company raised roughly $19 M in total, including a $9 M Series C in June 2020. After that round, momentum stalled. A planned Series D — intended to carry the company through 2021 — never closed. COVID-19 froze investor appetite, and by late 2021 Fabelio could no longer meet payroll. Employees filed a Change.org petition demanding months of unpaid wages. The company suspended operations in mid-2022. On 5 October 2022, the Commercial Court of Central Jakarta District Court (decision No. 47/Pdt.Sus PKPU/2022/PN.Niaga.JKT.PST) formally declared PT Kayu Raya Indonesia (Fabelio) bankrupt.

## Key decisions / turning points

- **Aggressive offline expansion:** 20 physical experience centres was a costly bet in a country where logistics and returns for bulky furniture are punishing — it amplified cash burn exactly when capital markets tightened.
- **Series D dependence:** The entire survival plan rested on one large round that never materialised. No bridge financing or cost-cutting plan came fast enough to extend runway.
- **Payroll failure:** Months of unpaid salaries turned a funding crisis into a public reputational collapse that made any rescue financing nearly impossible.

## Why it matters (pattern / lesson)

Fabelio is a textbook case of capital-intensity mismatch in furniture e-commerce: the sector requires warehousing, last-mile logistics, and physical showrooms, yet VC timelines and ticket sizes in Southeast Asia rarely support that burn rate beyond Series C. The COVID funding freeze arrived precisely when the company needed a large Series D, leaving no room to right-size the operation.
