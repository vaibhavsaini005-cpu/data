## One-liner

KiotViet, a Hanoi-based cloud POS and retail management SaaS, raised a $45 million Series B led by KKR in September 2021, becoming one of Vietnam's flagship SME-infrastructure success stories with 200,000+ business customers.

## What happened

Founded in Hanoi, KiotViet builds cloud-based point-of-sale and business management software for Vietnamese small and medium-sized enterprises — covering inventory, sales, staff scheduling, customer loyalty, and omnichannel operations across more than 20 retail sectors (food & beverage, pharmacy, fashion, electronics, etc.).

The company quietly scaled to over 200,000 active business customers by 2021, largely through word-of-mouth among micro and small retailers who previously relied on Excel spreadsheets or paper ledgers. In September 2021, KKR led a $45 million Series B with participation from existing investors Jungle Ventures (Singapore VC), Kasikorn Bank (Thailand), and family office Can Viet May. This was KKR's first direct venture investment in Vietnam.

## Key decisions / turning points

- **Vertical depth over breadth.** Rather than building generic horizontal SaaS, KiotViet built sector-specific modules (e.g., pharmacy stock tracking meeting Vietnamese regulatory requirements), which reduced churn and raised switching costs among its core SME users.
- **Mobile-first, affordable pricing.** Targeting micro-retailers with smartphone-based POS at low monthly subscription rates (rather than upfront hardware) dramatically widened the addressable market versus legacy POS vendors.
- **Tier-1 PE validation at Series B.** KKR's entry signalled that Vietnam's domestic SME-software market was large enough to attract global institutional capital, opening doors to future growth financing and international expansion.

## Why it matters (pattern / lesson)

KiotViet demonstrates the "infrastructure for the informal economy" playbook working in Southeast Asia: target a large population of unsophisticated SMEs, digitise a paper workflow they already do, price for local incomes, and compound through referrals. The KKR-led round also illustrates how Vietnam's growing middle-market is attracting PE attention that previously went only to Indonesia or Singapore.
