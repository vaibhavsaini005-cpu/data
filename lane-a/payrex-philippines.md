## One-liner

After co-founding and exiting PayMongo, Edwin Lacierda and Jaime Hing launched a second Philippine payments startup, PayRex, in late 2023 — which closed a $2M Series A from Kollab in January 2026 to fund its expansion.

## What happened

Edwin Lacierda (a Filipino lawyer and former Presidential Spokesperson under Benigno Aquino III) and Jaime Hing, having already co-founded fintech player PayMongo, launched a second, independent payments company, PayRex, in late 2023. PayRex built an end-to-end online payment platform for Philippine businesses — supporting credit/debit cards plus local e-wallets GCash and Maya, with integrations into Shopify, QR Ph, and BNPL provider Billease — positioning itself as a developer-friendly gateway for startups and SMEs. By January 2026, the company had signed clients including De La Salle–College of Saint Benilde, National University, SM Markets, and The SM Store, with SM Investments among its early backers. On January 26, 2026, digital transformation consultancy Kollab announced a $2 million Series A investment in PayRex (operating under the entity Evolut10n Labs, Inc.) to expand its merchant base and payment infrastructure.

## Key decisions / turning points

- **Founding a second, competing payments company after already building one (PayMongo)**: rather than staying inside their earlier venture, Lacierda and Hing chose to start over with a new company in the same sector.
- **Targeting developer- and SME-friendly integration** (Shopify, QR Ph, BNPL via Billease) as the wedge to differentiate from incumbent Philippine payment gateways.
- **Raising a Series A from a consultancy (Kollab) rather than a traditional VC fund**, explicitly framed by co-founder Jaime Hing as finding "a like-minded partner" for the next stage of the business.

## Why it matters (pattern / lesson)

PayRex is a case of repeat founders re-entering the exact market they'd already built a company in, betting that execution and product focus — not a new category — was the bigger opportunity. It's also a reminder that in emerging-market fintech, founder track record (Lacierda and Hing's PayMongo history) can be enough to attract institutional clients (SM Group entities, universities) and funding well before reaching the scale of the founders' first company.
