## One-liner

Sprout Solutions built an end-to-end HR and payroll platform for Philippine employers and grew it into a 180,000-user, 1,000-client business, closing a $10.7 million Series B in 2023 led by Cercano Management.

## What happened

Sprout Solutions is a Manila-based HR technology company offering an end-to-end platform covering payroll, HR management, and related workforce operations for employers across sectors including logistics, BPO, retail, entertainment, IT, financial services, real estate, and healthcare — industries with complex, high-volume compliance and payroll requirements in the Philippine market. The company built up its investor base gradually: a $1.6 million seed round and a $6 million Series A both closed in 2019, giving it runway to scale its client base through the early pandemic years when digitizing payroll and HR operations became a more urgent need for Philippine employers.

By April 2023, Sprout had grown to roughly 180,000 active platform users across more than 1,000 corporate clients. On April 18, 2023, the company announced a $10.7 million Series B round led by Cercano Management, with participation from SoftBank Ventures Asia, AFG Partners, GSR Ventures, Integra Partners, ACA Investments, and Mynavi Corp — the round marked the first Philippines investment for several of those firms and brought Sprout's total funding to $18.3 million.

## Key decisions / turning points

- **Vertical focus on Philippine HR/payroll compliance rather than a generic global HR tool**: Building specifically around local payroll rules and compliance requirements gave Sprout a defensibility that generic international HR SaaS products competing in the same market couldn't easily match.
- **Staged fundraising (seed and Series A both in 2019, Series B in 2023)**: Rather than raising a single large round early, Sprout scaled its customer base to 1,000+ clients before returning to raise a larger Series B, using growth metrics to justify a bigger round and a broader, more international investor syndicate.
- **Landing first-time Philippines bets from major regional funds**: The Series B being the first Philippines investment for firms like SoftBank Ventures Asia and GSR Ventures signaled that Sprout's traction was strong enough to pull in investors who hadn't previously backed anything in the country.

## Why it matters (pattern / lesson)

Sprout Solutions is a case study in winning a "boring but essential" vertical SaaS category — payroll and HR compliance — by going deep on one country's regulatory complexity instead of building a horizontal product from day one. Its funding path (small seed and Series A, then a multi-year gap before a larger Series B) also shows that steady, capital-efficient growth to a large multi-year customer base can be a more effective way to attract international investors than raising aggressively early on unproven metrics.
