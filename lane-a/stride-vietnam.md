## One-liner

Vietnamese residential solar platform Stride raised a $15 million Series B in May 2026, having nearly tripled its project portfolio since its Series A the year before.

## What happened

Stride, a Vietnamese cleantech company offering residential and small-to-medium-enterprise solar and battery systems on affordable payment plans with complimentary insurance, closed a $15 million Series B round reported on 22 May 2026. The round was led by Lightrock and TRIREC (via the Accelerate7 platform), with existing investors Clime Capital and UOB Venture Management also participating. Stride operates through a network of more than 75 engineering, procurement, and construction (EPC) partners nationwide.

## Key decisions / turning points

Since raising its Series A in 2025, Stride nearly tripled its project portfolio and expanded from a regional operation to nationwide coverage, positioning itself as Vietnam's largest residential and MSME solar platform. Building the round around EPC-partner distribution rather than owning installation crews directly let the company scale project volume faster than a vertically integrated model would have allowed.

## Why it matters (pattern / lesson)

Stride's growth tracks a broader shift in Vietnam's 2025-2026 funding environment toward climate and infrastructure-adjacent sectors seen as more resilient than consumer internet plays. Pairing an asset-light EPC-partner network with consumer-friendly financing (payment plans plus insurance) addressed two of residential solar's biggest adoption barriers — install capacity and upfront cost — at the same time, which is likely why investors backed a second consecutive round within about a year.
