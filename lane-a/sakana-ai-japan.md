## One-liner

Sakana AI, a Tokyo-based foundation model startup founded in 2023 by former Google researchers, raised a $135M Series B in November 2025 at a $2.65B valuation to keep building affordable generative AI models optimized for the Japanese language and culture.

## What happened

Sakana AI was founded in 2023 by Llion Jones, Ren Ito, and David Ha — Jones a co-author of the original "Attention Is All You Need" Transformer paper and Ha a former Google Brain researcher — with a specific strategic focus: building AI models that work well with smaller datasets and are optimized for Japanese language and cultural context, rather than competing head-on with the largest US labs on general-purpose scale.

The November 2025 $135M (¥20 billion) Series B, which valued the company at $2.65B post-money, reflected strong investor confidence in this differentiated positioning — building sovereign, culturally-adapted AI capability for Japan rather than importing US-developed general models wholesale. By the round, Sakana AI had become one of the highest-profile and most closely watched AI startups in Japan's ecosystem, competing for top-of-leaderboard funding position against robotics and autonomous-driving startups in the country's 2025 rankings.

## Key decisions / turning points

- Founded by researchers with deep foundational AI research credentials (a Transformer co-author, a former Google Brain researcher) rather than founders without core AI research background, lending significant technical credibility to the company's ambitions from inception.
- Explicitly targeted efficiency and cultural/linguistic optimization for Japan rather than attempting to out-scale US foundation model labs on raw parameter count or general-purpose capability, carving out a defensible strategic niche.
- Built specifically for smaller-dataset effectiveness, addressing a practical constraint many non-English-language AI applications face (less training data available than for English), rather than assuming access to internet-scale English-dominant datasets.
- Raised at a $2.65B valuation just over two years after founding, reflecting rapid capital-raising velocity for a foundation-model company outside the traditional US/China AI funding centers.

## Why it matters (pattern / lesson)

Sakana AI demonstrates that foundation-model startups outside the US and China can build defensible, well-funded businesses by explicitly optimizing for a specific language, culture, and data-availability context rather than trying to compete on raw scale with the largest labs — a strategy that turns a resource disadvantage (less available Japanese-language training data relative to English) into a specialization advantage. The founders' deep research pedigree also illustrates how technical credibility from prior foundational AI research can translate directly into investor confidence and funding velocity, even for a company barely two years old.
