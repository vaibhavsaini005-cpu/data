## One-liner

HungryGoWhere, Singapore's pioneering food-discovery and restaurant-reservation portal, shut down in July 2021 after 15 years, unable to withstand pandemic-era competition from food-delivery apps, before Grab bought the brand and relaunched it a year later.

## What happened

HungryGoWhere was founded in 2004 by Dennis Goh, Wong Hoong An and Tan Yung Yih, and launched publicly in 2006 as a website where diners could browse restaurant reviews, menus and locations, and later make table reservations. In May 2012, Singtel acquired the platform's parent company, GTW Holdings, for S$12 million and folded operations into its inSing.com subsidiary. In March 2017 HungryGoWhere added a food-takeaway ordering feature to compete with the rising wave of delivery apps.

That pivot wasn't enough. Grab, foodpanda and Deliveroo scaled aggressively through the late 2010s, and the Covid-19 pandemic then gutted the dine-in reservations business that remained HungryGoWhere's core identity. Singtel announced in June 2021 that the platform would cease operations, with a spokesperson citing "severe challenges" in the F&B tech industry compounded by the pandemic. The site went offline on 11 July 2021, ending a 15-year run.

Grab acquired the dormant HungryGoWhere brand and domain from Singtel and relaunched it in June 2022 as a food-and-lifestyle content hub feeding into Grab's own delivery ecosystem — the original standalone company and business model, however, was gone for good.

## Key decisions / turning points

- **Bolt-on ordering feature came too late (2017)**: adding takeaway ordering years after dedicated delivery apps had already captured the market did not change HungryGoWhere's competitive position.
- **Reservations-first identity became a liability in Covid-19**: a business built around discovering and booking dine-in restaurants had little to fall back on when in-person dining collapsed in 2020-2021.
- **Corporate parent chose to exit rather than re-invest**: Singtel, a telco rather than a tech-native operator, opted to wind the unit down instead of funding another pivot.
- **Brand outlived the company**: Grab's 2022 acquisition of the name shows the HungryGoWhere brand still had residual recognition value even after the original business failed commercially.

## Why it matters (pattern / lesson)

HungryGoWhere shows how a category-defining first mover (Singapore's first major food-discovery portal) can still be overtaken when it fails to fully commit to a business-model shift — its 2017 ordering feature was reactive, not a real strategic bet, while competitors built delivery-first from day one. It's also a case study in how large corporate parents (Singtel) will cut a legacy consumer-tech unit rather than keep subsidising it once its core use case (dine-in discovery) is structurally disrupted by both new entrants and an exogenous shock like Covid-19.
