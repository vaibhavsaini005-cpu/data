## One-liner

ZaloPay, VNG's e-wallet, pivoted from a pure QR-payments app into an "open payment platform" offering installment loans and investment products, after years of industry-leading losses on the old model.

## What happened

ZaloPay is the digital-wallet arm of VNG Corporation, built on top of Vietnam's Zalo messaging app. For years it competed as a straightforward e-wallet: QR payments, bill pay, and merchant transactions, funded by aggressive user-acquisition spending. That model came with a cost — ZaloPay recorded a loss of over 29.1 billion VND in 2023 (narrowing from 2022 but still the largest loss among Vietnamese e-wallets), even as total payment volume grew 27% and its QR merchant network passed 36,000 partners.

Facing intensifying competition from banks' own mobile apps and the national VietQR interbank payment system — both of which erode the pure-payments e-wallet's reason to exist — ZaloPay announced in mid-2024 that it would transition into an "open payment platform," layering financial services on top of payments rather than competing on payments alone. It introduced installment payments and a Buy Now, Pay Later product built with Lotte Finance and partner banks CIMB Vietnam and SHB Finance, offering cash loans up to 30 million VND at interest capped between 8% and 13% annually, targeted for rollout by Q3 2024. CEO Le Lan Chi framed the move as addressing the "diverse requirements" of its user base rather than just processing transactions.

## Key decisions / turning points

- **Stopped competing on payments volume alone.** Once VietQR let any bank app do interbank QR payments for free, ZaloPay's core value proposition as a standalone wallet weakened, forcing a shift toward services banks don't offer as easily.
- **Moved into lending rather than deepening payments.** Instead of doubling down on merchant acquisition, ZaloPay partnered with licensed lenders (Lotte Finance, CIMB Vietnam, SHB Finance) to add BNPL and quick loans — a materially different, credit-risk-bearing business from processing QR transactions.
- **Leveraged the existing Zalo network rather than building new distribution.** The pivot layered financial products onto an already-large user base inside the Zalo messaging ecosystem instead of trying to acquire a new customer base for lending.

## Why it matters (pattern / lesson)

ZaloPay's shift illustrates a common trajectory for e-wallets across Southeast Asia once national real-time payment rails (like VietQR) commoditize basic QR payments: wallets that don't own a bank charter or lending license have to partner their way into financial services to keep growing revenue per user, rather than relying on payments volume and subsidized user growth indefinitely.
