## One-liner

Timeless Events, the New Zealand promoter behind the Juicy Fest hip-hop and R&B music festival, was placed into voluntary liquidation in March 2025 after Auckland authorities declined its liquor licence application, forcing the festival's cancellation.

## What happened

Timeless Events operated as the New Zealand promoter of Juicy Fest, a touring hip-hop and R&B festival. The company's ability to run the event in Auckland depended on securing a liquor licence, a standard requirement for a large outdoor festival where alcohol sales are a significant part of both the customer experience and event revenue.

When Auckland authorities declined Timeless Events' liquor licence application, the New Zealand leg of Juicy Fest had to be cancelled outright. With the event's revenue no longer available to cover costs already committed against it (venue, marketing, artist bookings, and logistics), the company was placed into voluntary liquidation in March 2025.

## Key decisions / turning points

- Built the business around a single flagship annual event (Juicy Fest) rather than a diversified slate of smaller events, concentrating regulatory and financial risk into one licensing decision.
- Committed costs (venue, artists, marketing) ahead of final liquor licence approval, leaving the company exposed if the licence was denied.
- Chose voluntary liquidation once the licence denial made the event non-viable, rather than attempting to run a scaled-down, alcohol-free version of the festival.

## Why it matters (pattern / lesson)

Timeless Events illustrates a specific and often underestimated failure mode for event promoters: regulatory approval risk (here, a liquor licence) can be as fatal to a live-events business as a lack of ticket demand, especially when a company's revenue is concentrated in one flagship annual event and costs are locked in ahead of that approval. Diversifying across multiple smaller events, or sequencing spend commitments after — not before — licensing is secured, are the structural hedges this case highlights by their absence.
