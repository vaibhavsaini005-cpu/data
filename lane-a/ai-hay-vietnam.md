## One-liner

AI Hay, a Vietnamese consumer AI platform combining generative AI with social-network-style features, raised a $10M Series A led by Argor Capital in July 2025, bringing total funding to over $18M after growing to 15M+ downloads and 100M+ monthly queries since its May 2024 launch.

## What happened

AI Hay set out to bring free, hyperlocal AI tools to Vietnam's roughly 100 million citizens, wrapping generative AI knowledge-discovery features in a social-app-like interface rather than a bare chatbot. Founded by veterans of Vietnam's leading consumer tech platforms, the product launched in May 2024 and scaled quickly, reaching more than 15 million downloads and over 100 million questions answered per month by mid-2025 — fast enough growth to become, per its own framing, a daily habit for millions of Vietnamese users.

In July 2025 the company closed a $10M Series A led by Southeast Asia-focused VC firm Argor Capital, with participation from existing backers Square Peg, Northstar Ventures, AppWorks, and Phoenix Holdings, taking total capital raised to over $18M. The company said the funds would go toward expanding its web offering and building out AI-powered education, entertainment, and "AI companion" features for deeper daily-life integration.

## Key decisions / turning points

- Packaged generative AI as a social, habit-forming consumer product rather than a utility chatbot, targeting daily engagement instead of occasional lookup use.
- Targeted the mass Vietnamese-language market specifically (100M-person addressable population) rather than competing head-on with English-first global AI products.
- Built the founding team from alumni of Vietnam's existing large consumer tech platforms, giving the company distribution and product instincts from prior scaled Vietnamese apps.
- Used concrete usage metrics (15M+ downloads, 100M+ monthly queries) ahead of the raise to demonstrate real engagement rather than projected demand.

## Why it matters (pattern / lesson)

AI Hay shows that in large non-English-first markets, a hyperlocal, habit-oriented consumer AI product can out-compete global incumbents on engagement and attract serious Series A capital — even from investors outside the region — by proving daily usage at scale before emphasizing monetization. It also reflects a broader 2025 pattern in Vietnam's startup ecosystem: capital increasingly following AI and consumer-scale metrics over pure e-commerce or fintech plays.
