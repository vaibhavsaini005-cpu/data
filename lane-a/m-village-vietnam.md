## One-liner

Nguyen Hai Ninh, the former CEO of The Coffee House who founded serviced-apartment operator M Village in 2020, closed a $26 million round in August 2026 at more than double the company's previous valuation, tying the raise to a rebrand and restructuring into a multi-brand accommodation group.

## What happened

Ninh left his role as co-founder and CEO of Vietnamese coffee chain The Coffee House to build M Village, a modern serviced-apartment operator combining short- and long-term stays with workspaces, dining, wellness, and community facilities. The company opened its first property in 2022 and had scaled to 53 locations across Vietnam by mid-2026. In August 2026, M Village closed a $26 million round — $18 million from new lead investor Mizuho Asia Partners of Japan (via Grand Mia Pte. Ltd.) and $8 million from existing backer Trip.com, the Chinese online travel group that had first invested in mid-2024. The round priced shares at $9, up from $4.96 in the company's prior raise in March 2025, putting its post-money valuation at $121.4 million — more than double the $52.5 million it had been valued at less than a year and a half earlier. Alongside the raise, Ninh rebranded the company as Modern Village Lifestyle, positioning it to move beyond its original serviced-apartment format into a multi-brand portfolio spanning urban hotels and resort properties, a strategy he said was aimed at covering a wider range of market segments than the single-format business M Village started as.

## Key decisions / turning points

- 2020: Ninh founds M Village after his tenure as The Coffee House's co-founder and CEO, betting on a modern serviced-apartment format for Vietnam's urban rental market.
- Mid-2024: Trip.com takes an initial stake, becoming a strategic hospitality-tech partner alongside Vietnamese travel and property players.
- March 2025: M Village raises at $4.96/share, valuing the company at $52.5 million.
- August 2026: Ninh raises $26 million at $9/share ($121.4 million post-money) and simultaneously rebrands the company as Modern Village Lifestyle, shifting its strategy from a single serviced-apartment format to a multi-brand hotel and resort portfolio.

## Why it matters (pattern / lesson)

M Village is a case of a founder using a fundraise not just to scale the existing model but to explicitly re-position the company — pairing the capital raise with a name change and a stated shift from one accommodation format to a multi-brand portfolio, rather than treating the round as a straight scale-up of what already existed. The more-than-doubled valuation in under a year and a half, backed by both a hospitality-tech-oriented Japanese investor (Mizuho Asia Partners) and a repeat strategic investor (Trip.com), suggests the market read the repositioning as additive rather than a distress signal — a useful contrast to pivots that happen only after a model has visibly stopped working.
