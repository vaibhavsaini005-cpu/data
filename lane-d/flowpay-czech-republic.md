## One-liner

Flowpay, a Prague-based fintech founded in 2021 by William Jalloul and Daniel Hastík, secured €30 million in debt financing from London-based Fasanara Capital in March 2025 to accelerate its AI-driven lending and embedded finance products for SMEs across the Czech Republic, Slovakia, and the Netherlands.

## What happened

Flowpay built an AI-powered lending platform providing working capital, embedded finance, and risk assessment specifically for small and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs) and the lenders serving them, addressing a persistent financing gap where traditional banks often underserve smaller businesses due to higher perceived risk and lower-margin loan sizes. The company's AI-driven risk-assessment approach positioned it to evaluate SME creditworthiness more efficiently than conventional underwriting processes, letting it extend working-capital solutions to businesses that might otherwise struggle to access traditional bank financing.

The €30 million debt financing facility from Fasanara Capital — a London-based investment manager specializing in fintech debt financing — provided Flowpay with lending capital specifically earmarked to scale its working-capital solutions for SMEs, rather than equity capital for general operations. Company leadership explicitly framed the round around an ambition to become "the next Czech unicorn," and the funding was structured to enable the company to scale beyond its existing Czech Republic, Slovakia, and Netherlands markets at a significantly faster pace than would have been possible with its prior capital base alone.

## Key decisions / turning points

- Raised debt financing specifically structured as lending capital rather than equity, aligning the funding mechanism directly with the company's core lending business model rather than diluting founder and existing investor equity for growth capital.
- Focused specifically on SME lending and embedded finance rather than consumer lending or broader fintech products, addressing a segment with well-documented financing gaps relative to larger enterprise or consumer borrowers.
- Attracted a specialist fintech debt investor (Fasanara Capital) with specific expertise in lending-capital structures, rather than a generalist equity venture fund, matching investor expertise to the company's capital-intensive lending model.
- Explicitly targeted multi-country expansion (Czech Republic, Slovakia, Netherlands) as the immediate use of the new capital, rather than deepening its position in a single home market first.

## Why it matters (pattern / lesson)

Flowpay's debt-financing round illustrates how AI-driven SME lending fintechs can access specialized lending capital — rather than diluting equity — once they've demonstrated a credible AI-based risk-assessment approach, since specialist fintech debt investors like Fasanara Capital are specifically structured to provide capital for companies whose core business is itself extending credit. The company's simultaneous multi-country expansion (Czech Republic, Slovakia, Netherlands) funded by a single debt facility also demonstrates a capital-efficient growth pattern for lending-focused fintechs: rather than raising separate equity rounds for market entry, debt capital scaled appropriately to loan-book growth can fund geographic expansion directly tied to revenue-generating lending activity.
