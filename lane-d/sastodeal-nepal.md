## One-liner

On April 14, 2024, Sastodeal co-founder Amun Thapa confirmed that the Nepali e-commerce marketplace had shut down about three weeks earlier, eliminating 200 jobs after falling demand and delivery failures eroded a platform that had reached 10 million users.

## What happened

Sastodeal launched in 2011 as one of Nepal's earliest homegrown online marketplaces. It later attracted a $1 million investment from Dolma Impact Fund and formed cross-border partnerships with India's Myntra and Flipkart. By the time of its closure, The Kathmandu Post reported that the platform had served 10 million users.

Thapa said the company chose to stop operating because consumption had declined sharply and management saw no immediate sign of improvement. He also acknowledged that its customer base fell as Sastodeal failed to deliver orders on time in a highly competitive market. The shutdown cost 200 jobs, although Thapa left open the possibility that new management could eventually revive the business.

## Key decisions / turning points

- Built a broad marketplace and cross-border assortment through Myntra and Flipkart, but the partnerships did not overcome weaknesses in reliable fulfillment.
- Accepted $1 million from Dolma Impact Fund in 2020 and reached 10 million users, yet scale did not translate into resilience when discretionary consumption contracted.
- Thapa and the team decided in March 2024 to shut down rather than continue operating into a market with sharply lower demand and no near-term recovery in sight.
- Kept discussions about new management alive, making the closure a halt with a possible future restart rather than a claim that the brand had no residual value.

## Why it matters (pattern / lesson)

Sastodeal shows why user totals and marquee partnerships are weak substitutes for dependable execution in e-commerce. Once delayed or failed deliveries damage trust, a demand downturn exposes the problem quickly because customers can move to better-capitalized rivals. The 200-job loss also illustrates how operational fragility turns into a material employment outcome even for a nationally recognized platform.
