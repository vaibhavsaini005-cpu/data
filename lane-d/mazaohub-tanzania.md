## One-liner

MazaoHub, a Tanzanian agritech startup founded in 2021, raised a $2M oversubscribed pre-seed round in September 2025 — combining $1.5M equity led by Catalyst Fund with $500K non-dilutive funding — to scale its AI-powered soil intelligence and "Tech and Touch" farming model across Africa.

## What happened

MazaoHub built a hybrid model blending AI-driven soil intelligence with in-person agronomist support delivered through physical "Farmer Excellence Centres," directly addressing a common failure mode of purely digital agritech products in markets where farmers benefit from combining data-driven tools with trusted human guidance. The company's soil test kits, sensors, and digital tools are designed to help farmers boost yields, cut costs, and reduce chemical fertilizer use by up to 30%.

The September 2025 pre-seed round was notably oversubscribed and structured as a blend of $1.5 million in equity — led by Catalyst Fund with participation from Nordic Impact Fund, Mercy Corps Ventures, elea Foundation, Impacc, and DOB Equity — plus $500,000 in non-dilutive funding from the Livelihood Impact Fund. This blended capital structure, combining equity from impact-oriented VCs with grant-like non-dilutive capital, reflects a financing approach well-suited to agritech ventures serving smallholder farmers, where near-term unit economics may not yet support pure commercial equity investment alone.

## Key decisions / turning points

- Combined AI-powered technology (soil intelligence, sensors) with human agronomist support via physical "Farmer Excellence Centres" rather than a purely digital product, addressing farmers' need for trusted, hands-on guidance alongside data tools.
- Structured the pre-seed round as a blend of equity and non-dilutive funding rather than equity alone, matching the capital structure to the realistic near-term revenue profile of a smallholder-farmer-focused agritech business.
- Attracted a syndicate of impact-oriented investors (Catalyst Fund, Nordic Impact Fund, Mercy Corps Ventures) rather than generalist commercial VCs, aligning funding sources with the company's financial-inclusion and climate-resilience mission.
- Quantified concrete operational outcomes (up to 30% reduction in chemical fertilizer use) as part of its value proposition, giving both farmers and investors a measurable, sustainability-linked metric beyond generic yield claims.

## Why it matters (pattern / lesson)

MazaoHub demonstrates that a "Tech and Touch" hybrid model — combining AI-driven agricultural data tools with in-person agronomist support — can be more effective than purely digital approaches for reaching smallholder farmers, who often need trusted human guidance to act on technical recommendations rather than a data dashboard alone. The blended equity-plus-non-dilutive-funding capital structure is also a broadly reusable pattern for agritech ventures serving low-margin smallholder markets: pairing patient, impact-aligned equity with grant-like capital lets a company scale sustainably without requiring immediate commercial-scale unit economics.
