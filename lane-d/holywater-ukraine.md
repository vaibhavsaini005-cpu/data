## One-liner

HOLYWATER co-founders Bogdan Nesvit and Anatolii Kasianov launched the My Drama vertical-series app in March 2024; by December, the product was generating more than $20 million in annual revenue while the broader bootstrapped company reported $90 million in ARR.

## What happened

Nesvit and Kasianov began with $100,000, seven people, and consumer apps ranging from live wallpapers to fonts and video editors. After years of testing products, they concentrated HOLYWATER on media technology and built a portfolio around serialized stories. In March 2024 the founders launched My Drama, applying the short, mobile-native format of vertical video to scripted series.

Forbes Ukraine reported in December 2024 that My Drama was producing more than $20 million in annual revenue. Nesvit separately wrote that HOLYWATER had grown from zero to $90 million in annual recurring revenue without outside investment, while serving more than 20 million users. The company paired its product shift with clearer operating ownership: each product received a single accountable manager under a RACI structure, replacing diffuse responsibility across functional teams.

## Key decisions / turning points

- Nesvit and Kasianov moved from a broad portfolio of utility apps into serialized media products.
- Launched My Drama in March 2024 to capture the emerging vertical-series format on mobile.
- Assigned one product manager responsibility for each product's financial result through a RACI operating model.
- Bootstrapped the company instead of relying on external investment while scaling through wartime disruption.

## Why it matters (pattern / lesson)

HOLYWATER illustrates a disciplined product-portfolio pivot: experiment broadly, then concentrate resources once a format shows repeatable demand. The March 2024 launch and more than $20 million annual revenue reported within the same year give the decision a clear, time-bound outcome. Its accountability model also shows how organizational design can keep pace when a small founding team becomes a multi-product media company.
