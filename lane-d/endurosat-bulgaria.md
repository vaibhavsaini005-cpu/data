## One-liner

EnduroSat, a Sofia-based modular satellite manufacturer, raised a €43 million ($49 million) Series B led by Peter Thiel's Founders Fund in May 2025, bringing total funding to €85.72 million, to scale production of its Gen3 satellite platform and build one of Europe's largest space R&D centers.

## What happened

EnduroSat built a modular satellite manufacturing platform targeting the 200-500 kg satellite class, positioning itself as infrastructure for the growing small-satellite industry rather than a single-mission satellite operator. Investor Delian Asparouhov (Founders Fund) publicly described spending a decade searching for a "Dell for Satellites" — a standardized, modular satellite manufacturing platform — and expressed initial skepticism that a Bulgarian company would be the answer before backing EnduroSat's round.

The May 2025 €43 million Series B, led by Founders Fund, is earmarked to scale production of EnduroSat's Gen3 satellite platform and to fund a new 17,500 square meter facility in Sofia — described as set to become one of the largest space R&D centers in Europe, featuring satellite avionics labs, two assembly lines, and significantly expanded clean rooms. The round represents a landmark validation of Eastern European deep-tech capability in a capital-intensive frontier sector (space manufacturing) traditionally dominated by US and Western European players.

## Key decisions / turning points

- Built a modular, standardized satellite manufacturing platform (the "Dell for Satellites" model) rather than custom-building satellites mission-by-mission, positioning the company as reusable infrastructure for the broader small-satellite industry.
- Attracted a marquee US venture investor (Peter Thiel's Founders Fund) specifically focused on deep-tech and space, rather than relying only on regional European or government-linked capital, bringing both capital and international credibility.
- Invested Series B proceeds into major physical infrastructure (a 17,500 sq m Sofia facility) rather than purely software or design capability, reflecting the genuinely capital-intensive, hardware-manufacturing nature of the satellite business.
- Located its manufacturing scale-up in Bulgaria rather than relocating to a more established space-industry hub (such as the US or Western Europe), betting that Eastern European engineering talent and cost structure could support a globally competitive space-hardware business.

## Why it matters (pattern / lesson)

EnduroSat's raise demonstrates that Eastern European deep-tech ventures can attract top-tier US venture capital and compete in globally capital-intensive frontier industries like space manufacturing, challenging assumptions that such capability is concentrated only in Silicon Valley or Western Europe. The investor's own stated initial skepticism about a Bulgarian company solving the "Dell for Satellites" problem — followed by conviction after diligence — also illustrates how geographic bias can cause sophisticated investors to underestimate genuinely differentiated technical capability originating outside traditional innovation hubs.
