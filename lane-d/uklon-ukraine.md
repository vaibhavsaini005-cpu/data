## One-liner

Co-founder Dmytro Dubrovsky and Uklon's founders agreed to sell 97% of the Ukrainian mobility platform to Kyivstar for US$155.2 million in March 2025; by the second quarter of 2026, Uklon had 5.2 million customers and quarterly revenue was up 50.8% year over year.

## What happened

Uklon had built Ukraine's largest homegrown ride-hailing platform without external financing and expanded into deliveries, advertising and Uzbekistan. On March 19, 2025, Kyivstar signed an agreement to acquire 97% of the business. Dubrovsky said the founders chose the telecom operator because its capital and expertise could support new verticals and international expansion.

The US$155.2 million transaction closed on April 2 with Uklon kept as a separate business and its full team retained. Fifteen months later, Uklon reported US$32.8 million of quarterly revenue, up 50.8%, and US$12.5 million of EBITDA, up 35.5%. It served 5.2 million customers in the quarter, completed 43 million rides and 1.4 million deliveries, and had expanded into travel, commerce, autonomous-vehicle testing and electric scooters.

## Key decisions / turning points

- On March 19, 2025, Dubrovsky and the other founders accepted Kyivstar as a strategic majority owner rather than continuing Uklon's bootstrapped independence.
- The founders retained 3% collectively through co-founder Vitaliy Dyatlenko, while co-founders Serhiy Smus and Dyatlenko joined the supervisory board.
- Kyivstar closed the purchase of 97% on April 2 for US$155.2 million and retained the entire operating team under CEO Serhiy Hryshkov.
- In the second quarter of 2026, customers grew 8.2%, rides 4.5%, deliveries 25.9%, revenue 50.8% and EBITDA 35.5% year over year.

## Why it matters (pattern / lesson)

The sale paired a profitable local platform with a buyer that could distribute and finance adjacent digital services without absorbing the operating company. The post-close financial growth and new product launches provide a resolved outcome for the founders' choice of strategic ownership.
