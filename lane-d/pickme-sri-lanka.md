## One-liner

PickMe founder and CEO Jiffry Zulfer took Sri Lanka's homegrown mobility platform public in October 2024 after offering 43,474,179 shares at LKR 36 each; the LKR 1.56 billion offer was 2.7 times oversubscribed.

## What happened

Digital Mobility Solutions Lanka built PickMe into a localized ride-hailing and delivery platform with more than 75,000 drivers, 900,000 customers, and 3,000 merchants before the IPO. On September 5, 2024, Zulfer described the listing as a pivotal step intended to let public investors participate in the company's growth while reinforcing confidence in the brand's expansion.

The offer opened on September 13 with 43,474,179 existing ordinary voting shares priced at LKR 36, representing 13.04% of the company. Trading began on the Colombo Stock Exchange's Main Board on October 3. The CSE reported that the offer was oversubscribed 2.7 times, providing a concrete market test of demand for a locally built technology platform.

## Key decisions / turning points

- Zulfer and the board chose a domestic public listing after a decade of building, offering 13.04% of the company rather than pursuing only another private funding round.
- Used an offer for sale of existing shares, creating liquidity for shareholders while broadening public ownership.
- Built local-language support and a Sri Lanka-specific operating model, which management identified as advantages against global mobility competitors.
- Expanded beyond ride hailing into delivery and logistics before listing, giving investors exposure to a broader transaction platform rather than a single service.

## Why it matters (pattern / lesson)

PickMe is a rare public-market validation of a locally built platform in a smaller emerging economy. The oversubscription shows that domestic capital markets can provide a credible liquidity path when a technology company combines recognizable consumer reach, local operating advantages, and formal public-company governance. It also demonstrates the value of diversifying adjacent services before an IPO without abandoning the core platform.
