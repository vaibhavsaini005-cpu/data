## One-liner

Sufra AI, a Qatar-based restaurant-tech startup founded in 2025 by Ekaterina Demenkova and Jemal Velihanova, secured $100,000 in pre-seed funding in April 2026 as the first investment of Snoonu Startup Factory, a new venture-building initiative from Qatari super-app Snoonu, to build AI-powered smart menu systems that adapt to individual diners.

## What happened

Sufra AI built an AI-powered digital menu system for restaurants, letting diners scan a QR code to access a smart menu that adapts to their preferences, recommends personalized dish options, and enables seamless ordering, customization, and payment directly from the table — addressing the gap between static printed or PDF digital menus and a genuinely personalized dining experience. Beyond the consumer-facing personalization, the platform gives restaurants real-time insights into customer behavior and spending patterns, with the company citing potential to increase average order value by 20-30% through better-targeted recommendations.

The $100,000 pre-seed investment came from Snoonu, Qatar's prominent super-app for delivery and logistics, as the very first investment made through its newly launched Snoonu Startup Factory — a venture-building program explicitly designed to offer capital, mentorship, and operational support to early founders in the Qatari and broader Gulf startup ecosystem. Being selected as the flagship first investment for an established regional platform's new venture arm gave Sufra AI not just capital but a meaningful signal of validation and access to Snoonu's existing restaurant-industry relationships and operational expertise.

## Key decisions / turning points

- Built a specifically AI-personalization-focused digital menu product rather than a generic QR-code menu digitization tool, differentiating on the recommendation and behavioral-insight layer rather than just the underlying digitization.
- Became the first portfolio investment of Snoonu Startup Factory, a strategic positioning that gave the young company (founded just the year before) association with and support from an established regional consumer-tech platform.
- Targeted restaurants' data and revenue-optimization needs (order-value insights, personalized upsell recommendations) as a core value proposition alongside the consumer-facing menu experience, giving the product a clear B2B monetization angle beyond pure UX improvement.
- Launched in Qatar specifically, positioning within a Gulf restaurant-tech market where Snoonu's existing super-app infrastructure and restaurant relationships could provide meaningful go-to-market support for a young startup.

## Why it matters (pattern / lesson)

Sufra AI's selection as Snoonu Startup Factory's inaugural investment illustrates an emerging pattern in the Gulf startup ecosystem: established regional super-apps and consumer platforms are increasingly launching their own venture-building arms to back early-stage startups that complement their existing infrastructure and customer relationships, offering founders both capital and strategic access that a purely financial investor could not provide. The company's focus on AI-driven behavioral personalization and revenue optimization for restaurants — rather than just digitizing existing menus — also reflects a broader trend in restaurant tech toward monetizing data insights as a core product value proposition, not merely a byproduct of digitization.
