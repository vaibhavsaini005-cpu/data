## One-liner

Uruguay-founded cybersecurity startup Strike, which offers AI-driven continuous penetration testing for clients including Hitachi Vantara, Telefónica, and Santander, raised a $13.5M Series A led by FinTech Collective in March 2025 to expand in Brazil and the US and launch its Strike 360 automated testing platform.

## What happened

Strike was founded in 2022 in Uruguay to offer continuous penetration testing — a security-testing approach that simulates ongoing computer attacks against a system, network, or application to surface vulnerabilities on a rolling basis rather than through periodic, point-in-time audits. The company expanded from its Uruguay base to open offices in Argentina, Brazil, and the United States, building a client roster that includes Hitachi Vantara, Farmacias del Ahorro, Telefónica, Santander, and Clip.

The March 2025 $13.5M Series A was led by FinTech Collective, with participation from Galicia Ventures, Greyhound Capital Management, FJ Labs, Canary Ventures, and Carao Ventures. The capital is earmarked for accelerating development of an AI-driven, automated security-testing engine covering the full pentesting lifecycle from vulnerability discovery through remediation, and for launching Strike 360, a platform combining AI-powered attack simulations with automated retesting designed to cut vulnerability-detection times from months to seconds. The company set a public target of automating 50% of its pentesting process and surpassing eight-figure annual recurring revenue by the end of 2025.

## Key decisions / turning points

- Built the core product around continuous, ongoing testing rather than traditional periodic penetration-testing engagements, addressing a structural gap where point-in-time security audits leave systems unmonitored between assessments.
- Expanded regionally from Uruguay into Argentina, Brazil, and the US early in its growth, building a multi-country enterprise client base (including major banks and telecoms) rather than staying confined to a single home market.
- Prioritized AI-driven automation of the pentesting workflow itself (discovery through remediation) as the primary product investment from the new funding, aiming to fundamentally compress detection timelines rather than simply scaling headcount-based testing services.
- Set explicit, public, time-bound growth targets (50% pentesting automation, eight-figure ARR by end of 2025) tied directly to the funding round, creating clear investor and market accountability for the capital's intended use.

## Why it matters (pattern / lesson)

Strike demonstrates that Latin American-founded cybersecurity companies can win enterprise trust from major regional and global institutions (banks, telecoms) by combining automation-driven speed with credible security expertise, competing directly against established Western cybersecurity vendors on capability rather than price alone. Building continuous, AI-automated testing as the core differentiator — rather than a services-heavy consulting model — also positions the company for software-like margins and scalability that traditional penetration-testing firms structurally cannot match.
