## One-liner

ueno bank, Paraguay's leading digital bank with more than 3 million customers, priced a $350 million international senior notes offering in March 2026 — the largest-ever international bond issuance by a Paraguayan bank — with demand 1.5 times oversubscribed.

## What happened

ueno bank built a digital banking platform serving Paraguay, growing to over 3 million customers and processing more than 70 million monthly transactions, a substantial scale for the country's banking market. The bank's growth accelerated following its acquisition of Vision Banco and subsequent incorporation in June 2024, consolidating its position as a leading force in Paraguay's digital banking transformation.

The $350 million offering — launched February 26, 2026, priced March 3, and closed March 6, 2026 — was structured as five-year Senior Unsecured Notes carrying a 6.70% coupon, issued under Rule 144A/Regulation S and governed by New York law, with BofA Securities and J.P. Morgan Securities as joint bookrunners. The deal represented ueno's inaugural international debt offering and the largest such issuance ever completed by a Paraguayan bank, drawing investor demand 1.5 times the amount offered — a strong signal of international capital markets' confidence in ueno's business model and Paraguay's broader financial sector. The bank had separately invested $120 million over the preceding 18 months to strengthen its technology infrastructure and develop new financial products.

## Key decisions / turning points

- Pursued growth through acquisition (Vision Banco, 2024) rather than purely organic expansion, rapidly consolidating market position and customer base in Paraguay's digital banking sector.
- Chose an international bond issuance (Rule 144A/Reg S, New York law, Wall Street underwriters) rather than relying solely on domestic Paraguayan capital markets, tapping a much larger and more liquid international investor base for growth capital.
- Invested heavily in technology infrastructure ($120 million over 18 months) ahead of and alongside the bond raise, building the operational capacity needed to support continued customer and transaction volume growth.
- Structured the debt offering as the bank's inaugural international issuance specifically to establish a track record and pricing benchmark in global capital markets, setting up potential future offerings.

## Why it matters (pattern / lesson)

ueno bank demonstrates that a digital bank from a smaller Latin American market (Paraguay) can access large-scale international capital markets directly, rather than remaining dependent on smaller domestic funding pools, once it has built sufficient scale (3 million+ customers) and a credible growth and acquisition track record. The oversubscribed demand for Paraguay's first major international bank bond issuance also signals that global fixed-income investors are willing to extend meaningful capital to well-run financial institutions in less-covered Latin American markets, not just the region's largest economies (Brazil, Mexico, Argentina).
