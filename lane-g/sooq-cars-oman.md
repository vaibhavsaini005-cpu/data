## One-liner

Sooq Cars, an Omani vehicle-trading marketplace with a user base exceeding one million spanning ready stock, imports, spare parts, and leasing across Oman and the wider Gulf, secured seed investment from Muscat-based Cyfr Capital in partnership with Future Fund Oman in August 2026, as one of five Omani startups backed in the round.

## What happened

Sooq Cars built a comprehensive vehicle-trading platform covering the full car-ownership lifecycle rather than a single transaction type — spanning ready-stock vehicle sales, vehicle imports, spare parts, and leasing — positioned to serve Oman's automotive market alongside the wider Gulf region. The platform's user base exceeding one million reflects meaningful market penetration in a sector where trust and verification are historically significant friction points for online vehicle transactions, particularly for imports and used-vehicle sales.

The investment came through Cyfr Capital's Seed Fund, deployed in collaboration with Future Fund Oman (FFO) — Oman's sovereign investment vehicle focused on driving economic diversification — as part of a five-company cohort that also included food delivery platform iO Eats, tourism marketplace Darrbak, AI and data company Decoil, and packaging provider Pack'N. Cyfr Capital did not disclose the specific investment amount, the total Seed Fund size, or the equity stakes acquired, though the joint deployment with a state-backed investment vehicle reflects Oman's broader strategic push to build a more diversified, technology-enabled private sector.

## Key decisions / turning points

- Built a full-lifecycle vehicle-trading platform (ready stock, imports, parts, leasing) rather than a narrower single-transaction-type marketplace, capturing multiple revenue touchpoints across a customer's ownership journey.
- Expanded coverage beyond Oman into the wider Gulf region rather than remaining a purely domestic Omani platform, positioning for a larger regional addressable market from a relatively early stage.
- Achieved a user base exceeding one million before this funding round, demonstrating substantial market traction and trust-building in a transaction category (vehicle trading) where online trust is historically a significant adoption barrier.
- Secured investment through a fund structured as a partnership between private venture capital (Cyfr Capital) and Oman's sovereign development vehicle (Future Fund Oman), aligning private capital deployment with national economic diversification priorities.

## Why it matters (pattern / lesson)

Sooq Cars demonstrates that automotive marketplace platforms addressing genuine trust and verification friction in vehicle trading — a notoriously difficult category for online commerce — can achieve substantial user scale (over one million users) even in a smaller Gulf market like Oman, provided the platform covers enough of the ownership lifecycle to become a default destination for automotive needs. The joint deployment structure between Cyfr Capital and Future Fund Oman also illustrates a broader Gulf-region pattern: sovereign investment vehicles increasingly partner with private venture funds to co-invest in early-stage technology companies, using private-sector deal sourcing and diligence expertise while directing state capital toward economic diversification goals.
