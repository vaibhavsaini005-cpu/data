## One-liner

Data4Sales co-founder and CEO Joaquín Sosa decided in April 2024 to use a completed US$2.3 million seed round to deepen the Uruguayan startup's regional presence and enter Brazil after revenue had grown more than 900% in 2023.

## What happened

Sosa and Alexandre Chaves began Data4Sales as a tool for online stores to understand customer purchasing patterns. They expanded the product beyond e-commerce data to unify physical-store, marketplace, and mobile-app transactions, using predictive AI to drive retention and repeat sales.

On April 10, 2024, Sosa described the next allocation decision after closing the second US$1.2 million tranche of a US$2.3 million seed round: reinforce three of the seven Latin American markets already served and expand into Brazil. At that point, Data4Sales employed 16 people across seven countries, served about 100 customers, and reported more than 900% revenue growth in 2023.

## Key decisions / turning points

- The founders broadened Data4Sales from an e-commerce-only tool into an omnichannel customer-data and predictive-marketing platform.
- Sosa chose to deploy the April 2024 seed capital toward deeper market share in three existing countries and entry into Brazil.
- The company supported a regional model with a distributed 16-person team rather than limiting the product to Uruguay.
- The measurable base for the expansion was approximately 100 customers in seven countries following more than 900% revenue growth in 2023.

## Why it matters (pattern / lesson)

Data4Sales shows how a small-market startup can earn the right to expand by first broadening the product around customer data that retailers already generate. The decision to enter Brazil followed significant revenue growth and multi-country adoption, making the financing an accelerator for an established regional pattern rather than a substitute for traction.
