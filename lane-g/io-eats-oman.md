## One-liner

iO Eats, an Omani food delivery platform operating cloud kitchens across six locations and letting users order from more than 30 restaurants in a single basket, received seed investment from Cyfr Capital's Seed Fund in partnership with Future Fund Oman, as one of five startups backed in the fund's August 2026 investment round.

## What happened

iO Eats built a food delivery platform centered on cloud kitchens spanning six locations, with a distinctive multi-restaurant basket feature allowing a single order to combine dishes from more than 30 different restaurants — addressing a common frustration in food delivery where consumers wanting variety must otherwise place multiple separate orders with different delivery fees and wait times. By operating its own cloud kitchen infrastructure rather than relying solely on partnerships with independent restaurants' existing kitchens, the company gained more direct control over fulfillment consistency and delivery logistics.

The seed investment came through Cyfr Capital's Seed Fund, deployed in collaboration with Future Fund Oman (FFO), as one of five simultaneous investments the fund made across different sectors of Oman's startup ecosystem — alongside automotive marketplace Sooq Cars, tourism marketplace Darrbak, AI and data company Decoil, and packaging and storage provider Pack'N. This coordinated multi-startup deployment reflects a fund strategy of building a diversified early-stage portfolio across Oman's emerging startup sectors in a single investment cycle, rather than concentrating capital in a single company or sector.

## Key decisions / turning points

- Built proprietary cloud kitchen infrastructure across six locations rather than depending entirely on partner restaurants' own kitchens, gaining greater control over fulfillment quality and consistency.
- Introduced a multi-restaurant single-basket ordering feature, differentiating on customer convenience by removing the need for multiple separate orders when customers want dishes from different restaurants.
- Attracted investment as part of a coordinated, multi-sector seed fund deployment (alongside four other Omani startups) rather than a standalone funding round, benefiting from Cyfr Capital and Future Fund Oman's broader ecosystem-building strategy.

## Why it matters (pattern / lesson)

iO Eats illustrates how food delivery platforms can differentiate in increasingly saturated global delivery markets by building genuinely novel ordering mechanics — like combining multiple restaurants into a single basket — rather than competing purely on delivery speed or restaurant selection breadth alone. The company's inclusion in Cyfr Capital's simultaneous five-startup seed deployment also demonstrates a distinctive early-stage ecosystem-building pattern in smaller Gulf markets like Oman: rather than funds evaluating and backing startups purely on an individual, sequential basis, coordinated multi-sector seed deployments can accelerate the formation of a broader domestic startup portfolio within a single fund cycle.
