## One-liner

COFE App, a Kuwaiti coffee-delivery and marketplace platform founded in 2017 by Ali Al Ebrahim, raised a $15M Series B in March 2023, bringing its total funding to $28.2M across three rounds, by connecting independent coffee brands and local/international franchise chains directly with customers for online ordering and delivery.

## What happened

COFE App built a mobile marketplace focused specifically on coffee, connecting independent local coffee shops and both regional and international franchise chains with customers who order for delivery or pickup, rather than operating as a generic food-delivery platform. This category-specific focus let the company build deeper merchant relationships and a more tailored customer experience around a single, high-frequency product category than a horizontal food-delivery competitor could.

The March 2023 $15M Series B round brought COFE's cumulative funding to $28.2M raised across three rounds since the company's 2017 founding, reflecting sustained investor confidence through multiple funding cycles in a Gulf market (Kuwait) that receives comparatively less venture attention than UAE or Saudi Arabia. The capital was earmarked for expanding COFE's merchant network and geographic footprint, building on its position as a category-focused vertical marketplace in the Gulf coffee and beverage space.

## Key decisions / turning points

- Focused specifically on coffee as a vertical rather than building a general food-delivery platform, allowing deeper merchant integration with both independent shops and larger franchise chains simultaneously.
- Built the platform to serve both independent local coffee brands and international franchise chains on the same marketplace, capturing supply-side diversity rather than specializing in only one merchant type.
- Sustained three funding rounds over roughly six years (2017-2023) to reach $28.2M in cumulative funding, reflecting a steady, multi-cycle capital-raising trajectory rather than a single large early raise.
- Chose Kuwait as a home base and initial focus market, a smaller and less venture-saturated Gulf market than the UAE or Saudi Arabia, before presumably pursuing regional expansion.

## Why it matters (pattern / lesson)

COFE App demonstrates that a narrowly-scoped, category-specific marketplace (coffee, specifically) can sustain multiple funding rounds and investor confidence in a Gulf market by building deep merchant-side relationships across both independent and franchise segments, rather than needing to compete horizontally across all food and beverage delivery. Its Kuwait origin also illustrates that venture-backed consumer platforms can take root and scale successfully outside the more heavily covered UAE/Saudi Arabia axis of Gulf startup activity.
