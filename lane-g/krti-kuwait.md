## One-liner

Kuwait-based fintech Krti, founded in 2022 by Abdulrahman Al Hammadi, Naser Boresli, and Abdullah AlBaker, closed a $1.5M pre-seed round from Core Vision Investment in October 2024 to launch its payment and card-loyalty platform for online merchants and shoppers in Kuwait and Saudi Arabia.

## What happened

Krti built payment solutions aimed at both sides of the e-commerce transaction: merchants seeking easier online payment infrastructure and shoppers seeking better value from their existing bank cards, via a loyalty program layered on top of standard card payments designed to maximize the benefits shoppers already have access to but may not fully utilize. This dual-sided approach positioned Krti as infrastructure for the e-commerce ecosystem rather than a single-purpose payment gateway or standalone loyalty app.

The October 2024 $1.5M pre-seed round came from Core Vision Investment (based in Saudi Arabia) under Kuwait's Financial Academy Financial Technology Investment Programme — a government-linked fintech investment initiative — with the round itself finalized in Silicon Valley. The funding was earmarked specifically to fund Krti's market launch across both Kuwait and Saudi Arabia simultaneously, targeting the broader GCC e-commerce payments opportunity rather than a single-country rollout.

## Key decisions / turning points

- Built a dual-sided value proposition (payment infrastructure for merchants, card-benefit maximization loyalty for shoppers) rather than a single-sided product, aiming to capture value and engagement from both parties in each e-commerce transaction.
- Raised through a government-linked fintech investment programme (Kuwait's Financial Academy Financial Technology Investment Programme) rather than purely private venture capital, tapping state-backed innovation infrastructure specifically designed to support early-stage Kuwaiti fintechs.
- Targeted simultaneous launch across two GCC markets (Kuwait and Saudi Arabia) from inception rather than proving the model in a single home market first, betting on shared regional payment infrastructure and consumer behavior to justify the parallel rollout.

## Why it matters (pattern / lesson)

Krti's raise illustrates how government-linked fintech investment programmes in the Gulf are becoming a meaningful early-stage capital source for local founders, complementing rather than replacing traditional venture capital and specifically targeting the kind of pre-seed stage that private investors often consider too early or too regionally narrow. Pairing merchant-side payment infrastructure with consumer-side loyalty incentives in a single product is also a reusable positioning for fintechs entering e-commerce payments: it creates two independent adoption motives (merchant cost/conversion benefits, shopper reward benefits) rather than relying on just one side of the market to drive growth.
